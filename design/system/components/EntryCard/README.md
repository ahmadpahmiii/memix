# EntryCard

The two big Home cards that open the two editors: Video meme and Photo meme.

- **Provide:** the editor it opens, a title in `display` (uppercase Anton) and a one-line description.
- **Colors:** Video meme on `primary` with `on-primary` text (the screen's one blue action); Photo meme on `surface-raised` with `text` and a `primary` icon.
- **Layout:** side by side in a two-column grid with `space-3` gap and `radius-lg`. No outline or shadow.
- **Don't:** add a third card or a second blue card on Home.
