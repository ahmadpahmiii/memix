- 2026-10-09 · P1-08 · Freesound is now open to cloud sessions (freesound.org, cdn.freesound.org; owner). On the page HTML, the license is `<a title="Go to the full license text" …>Creative Commons 0</a>` and the public HQ preview is `data-static-file-url` (`…-hq.mp3`, MP3 128 kbps). Originals need a login. Decoded preview length matches the page duration. Parser and analysis helpers lived in the session scratchpad (`fs-tools/`), not the repo.
- 2026-10-09 · P1-08 · The silence trim must be relative to each clip's peak (peak − 60 dB, floor −90). A fixed −60 dBFS cut the tail of a quiet source (boing peaks at −22 dBFS and needs +22.5 dB). With no limiter, the peak cap leaves transient-heavy sounds quiet: drum roll ended at −23.7 LUFS. If P1-08 finds it too quiet, add a look-ahead limiter option.
- 2026-10-09 · P1-08 · ffmpeg 6.1.1 AAC encoding via `normalize.sh` is byte-identical on re-run, so `sha256Output` is reproducible from the preview plus args. Starter `id` = uuid5(NAMESPACE_URL, "https://freesound.org/s/<id>/"), reused as `sounds.id` at P3-13.
- 2026-10-09 · P1-08 · An agent can't do the "listen" step. Objective substitutes: comparing pitch and tempo against a suspected source (a resample shifts both by the same ratio), and checking spectral peaks in gaps for a music bed. Still ask a person to listen, and say so.
- 2026-10-08 · P1-08 · Audio loudness: for clips under about 1 s, ffmpeg `loudnorm` pass-1 `input_i` read 7.6 LU lower than `ebur128` (−21.9 vs −14.3 on a 0.6 s transient). Intake should measure with `ebur128` (pad short clips to 3 s with `apad=whole_dur=3`) and apply a linear gain of min(−16 − I, −1.5 − TP). AAC adds about 0.5 dB of true-peak overshoot. Scripts tested in the session scratchpad (starter-sounds-tools).
- 2026-10-08 · P1-08 · Cloud sessions can't reach Wikimedia, OpenGameArt, Pixabay, Mixkit, Kenney, creativecommons.org or web.archive.org (proxy 403; Freesound was opened on 9 Oct). WebSearch with `allowed_domains: ["freesound.org"]` returns per-page license and file details. The owner prefers text license snapshots in the repo over Wayback captures.
- 2026-10-08 · P1-08 · The starter pack must be CC0. There is no in-app credit surface until P3-03 (sound details) and P5-07 (Credits), and CC-BY 4.0 also binds users who share their exports. Pixabay (standalone ban), Mixkit ("can't redistribute … in a tool") and ZapSplat (no apps or soundboards) don't qualify.
