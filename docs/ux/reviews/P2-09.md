# Design review: P2-09 (WhatsApp sticker packs)

Reviewed 8 Nov 2026 from 35 desktop renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P2-09/` (stand-in numbered stickers and fonts), the spec, the builder's notes and the WhatsApp README summary in the spec. Nothing ran on a device, and WhatsApp was not available, so the real intent, the "is added" check, WhatsApp Business and the Done when (a pack installs into WhatsApp on a test phone) are for the owner's phone.

## Verdict: Changes requested (two must-fixes)

## What works
- **The Stickers sheet row** (`99-*`): "WhatsApp packs" with the `sticker` icon and a chevron, the subtitle changing with the state ("2 packs", "Make a pack from your stickers", "Add 1 more...") above the grid.
- **Packs list** (`09-packs-list`): rows with the tray, the name (two lines, never cut), "12 stickers · In WhatsApp", dividers, the one blue New pack at the bottom in thumb reach; the long-press Menu, the 10-pack explanation and the Undo toast.
- **Builder** (`10-*`): the name field with the legal helper line, a three-column grid, selection as the white ring with a check disc, the counter ("3 selected") and the two buttons (Emojis a third, Add to WhatsApp two thirds, the one blue), the 31st-tap and under-3 toasts, a missing-file tile.
- **Too detailed** (`11-*`): a red "!" disc on the tile and the notice above the counter; **preparing** (`12`): the text "Getting your pack ready · 12 of 30" with a determinate bar, the grid inert; **not added, no WhatsApp, WhatsApp old, update** (`13-*`): calm notices, the primary label becomes "Update in WhatsApp" for a pack added before.
- **Emojis** (`20-*`): the list with a default 🙂 per sticker and a Done; the picker's 48 emojis in a 6-column grid with white selected tiles, the fourth-emoji toast.
- **Added** (`30-added`): the tray on `surface-raised`, the green "Added to WhatsApp" pill, the instruction with the pack's name, Done (the blue). Hindi at 200% for the builder, Added and the need-3 state.

## The builder's four deviations
1. **Back and Done return to the editor with the Stickers sheet closed: accepted.** The pack flow is its own full-screen set; reopening a sheet from a screen would need a result channel for one saved tap. Spec amended (Packs section): said once, so it doesn't read as a bug.
2. **Last pack deleted: the page stays with an empty list.** An empty list with only New pack is a blank screen. Decided: the page stays (jumping to the builder after 8 seconds would be worse) and shows an empty state, "No packs yet" and "Make a pack from your stickers.", with New pack. Undo brings the pack back. Spec amended; two strings (`packs_empty_title`, `packs_empty_body`) in five languages. Polish P1.
3. **At 200% the stacked bar plus the notice takes over half the screen: yes, fix it (M1).**
4. **Edited pack: stickers deleted from My stickers are dropped from its selection: no, that's M2.**

## Must fix
- **M1. The pinned bottom bar swallows the grid at 200% font** (`40-spanish-200-builder`). With a notice (five lines in Spanish: "1 sticker es demasiado detallado...") the pinned part is the notice, the counter and two stacked buttons, over half the window; the grid shows half of one row. Selecting stickers is the whole job of this screen. Fix (spec amended, Builder → Large font): the notice leaves the pinned bar and sits in the scrolling content just above the counter; the pinned part is only the counter and the buttons; if even that passes 40% of the window, the counter scrolls too and only the buttons stay pinned. The grid keeps at least a row and a half in view.
- **M2. Editing a pack silently drops stickers that were deleted from My stickers.** The spec's rule is that deleting from My stickers never breaks a pack: the pack keeps its own prepared file and the builder shows it selected, so the user sees what is in the pack. Dropping it means that pressing Update in WhatsApp removes a sticker the user never chose to remove, with no word. Fix: keep it (spec amended), shown from the pack's copy; the user can unselect it. If the build can't do that in this ticket, the minimum is a notice at the top on open: "1 sticker was deleted from My stickers, so it isn't in this pack any more." and the handoff says so.

## Polish
- **P1. The empty Packs state** (above): add the two strings and the screen.
- **P2. The Emojis picker's back arrow sits alone on its own row** under the title (`20-emojis-picker`), costing about 60 dp and reading like a second close. Put the back arrow at the start of the title row ("Emojis for this sticker"), where the close X is at the end; the picker then gains a row of emojis in view.
- **P3. The "!" disc is clipped at 200%** (`40-spanish-200-builder`: a bar with no dot on the red disc). Make the disc 24 dp with the glyph centered and never cropped by the tile's ring; or draw the mark as an icon.
- **P4. The title at 200% crowds the top edge** (`40-spanish-200-builder`, "NUEVO PACK" touches the top). The screen's top bar needs the status bar inset and at least `space-2` above the title.
- **P5. The Undo toast covers the screen title** (`09-packs-delete-toast`): fine while transient; the same one-width rule as the other toasts (P2-14 P3).
- **P6. "In WhatsApp" is shown only when WhatsApp reports it.** When WhatsApp is absent, the row shows nothing, which is right; confirm on the device that the check doesn't make the list wait.

## Fix check (9 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** `40-spanish-200-builder`: the five-line notice scrolls with the grid, the pinned part is the counter and two stacked buttons (about 32% of the window, under the 40% rule), and the grid shows about a row and a half above the notice. Accepted: that is the minimum I set. A pack still can't be picked comfortably in Spanish at 200% on 360 × 640, but nothing is cut and the content scrolls; the notice is rare (only after a failed size check).
- **M2 fixed.** Stickers deleted from My stickers stay selected from the pack's own copy.
- **Empty Packs state, P2 and P3 fixed.** `09-packs-empty-after-delete` has the empty state; the Emojis picker's back arrow now shares the title row (`20-emojis-picker`, the Sheet's `titleLeading` slot: fine, reusable); the "!" mark is a bar and a dot.
- **P4: confirm on a device.** The title now clears the top edge in the render; the real inset depends on the status bar, so QA checks it at 200% on a phone.
- **P5: accepted.** The Undo action on its own line is the Toast component's layout when the message wraps; it keeps Undo 48 dp tall and in reach. No change.
- **Tokens added** (both token files, DESIGN_SYSTEM): `pack-row-height` 72, `emoji-row-height` 64, `pack-tray-size` 96, `grid-tile-min` 96, `nav-row-height` 56, `sheet-max-height-fraction` 0.7, `pinned-bar-max-fraction` 0.4. Kotlin: `MemixSize.packRowHeight`, `emojiRowHeight`, `packTraySize`, `gridTileMin`, `navRowHeight`, `sheetMaxHeightFraction` (Float), `pinnedBarMaxFraction` (Float).

## Final verdict: Pass with polish
No must-fix items remain. The two device checks (the real WhatsApp install, and the top inset at 200%) are for the owner's phone.

## For the device pass (the owner's phone)
- The Done when: a pack of 3 and a pack of 30 install into WhatsApp (and WhatsApp Business, and both installed: the chooser), the tray icon, the emojis, one sticker sent; an edit and Update refresh the pack.
- Not added (cancel in WhatsApp), WhatsApp not installed, too-big sticker (a noisy picture), 10 packs.
- Prepared files: 512 × 512 WebP at 100 KB or less, tray 96 × 96 at 50 KB or less; the provider is readable only by WhatsApp.
- Hindi and Spanish at 200%: the builder with the notice (M1), the Emojis sheet, Packs and Added.
- LeakCanary after five builds.
