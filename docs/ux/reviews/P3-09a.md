## Verdict: Fail (two small must-fixes, one re-render)

The design is right and the build follows it: tokens only, no outline, shadow or gradient, no blue action, the 09b tabs absent (no placeholders), five languages present and matching the spec. It fails on two small things: the panel does not fit two sliders where it can (my spec's budget was wrong again, and one ToolPanel gap is avoidable), and the Hindi seconds unit I specified means the wrong word.

## Must-fixes

1. **Two sliders must show together on a 360 x 800 phone (flag 1).** Real budget in my review of the numbers (now in the spec and the ToolPanel README): a Slider row is about 68 dp (label line + 48 dp rail), not the 54 I assumed; the region is 229 dp at 360 x 640, 302 at 360 x 800, 348 at 412 x 915. With padding (32), title row (48), tabs (48) and the gap under them (16) plus the 16 dp gap between title row and tabs, only 69 / 142 / 188 dp are left: the 800 dp phone, the commonest size, misses two sliders (152) by 10 dp. **Fix:** in `ToolPanel`, no gap between the title row and the `pinned` part (both are 48 dp targets, the tab underline separates them). That gives 85 / 158 / 204: both sliders fit from 360 x 800 up, with 6 dp spare at 800. At 360 x 640 the second slider scrolls and I accept that (it is the floor size; ToolPanel's scroll rule). **Re-render** `panel-tone` and `panel-fades` at 360 x 800 (both sliders visible) and keep the 640 ones. If 800 still misses, tell me the measured numbers and I accept scrolling there too. Breaks: spec Layout intro (as corrected), ToolPanel README → Pinned slot.
2. **Hindi `fx_seconds` is "{n} से" (spec Copy, my error).** "से" is the postposition "from/than", not seconds; a screen reader says "1.2 se" and a reader sees a broken sentence. **Fix:** `{n} सेकंड` in `values-hi/strings.xml` (budget raised to 10). Spec corrected. Breaks: spec Copy (`fx_seconds`), Accessibility (spoken slider values).

## Answers to the builder's flags

1. **Two sliders in the 229 dp region:** my budget mistake, the same one as P3-08. The truth: two sliders never fit under a title row at 640 (even Volume-style: 32 + 48 + 16 + 152 = 248 > 229), and not at 800 with tabs and the old gap. Decision: **both** a spec fix (the budget table, the one-slider-at-640 acceptance, now in the spec and README) **and** the one layout change in must-fix 1. No new token, no change to the Slider. QA: Fade out is the control a person looks for in task 3 of the test script; watch whether people on a small phone find it under the fold (add "found Fade out without help" to the notes).
2. **ToolPanel README `pinned` slot:** written (`design/system/components/ToolPanel/README.md`: the slot, the no-gap rule, the 40% cap, the budget arithmetic).
3. **`a11y_audio_effects_reset` = "Audio effects reset":** approved. It is spoken only (no visible toast), polite, and matches "Reset all audio effects" in wording. Translations in the files are right: id "Efek audio direset", es "Efectos de audio restablecidos", pt "Efeitos de áudio redefinidos", hi "ऑडियो इफ़ेक्ट रीसेट हो गए". Added to the spec's Copy table.
4. **Heard after release; no haptic at a fade's limit; bars follow Volume on release.**
   - *After release, not while dragging:* a real step back from the spec's "fast phone is live" and from the Volume panel next to it, which is heard live. Accepted for 09a as a provisional limit, **not** as the final design. It is the PM's to schedule (a debounced live preview, about 250 ms after the last move, would cover most of it). What I need from QA on the owner's phone: how long from release to the new sound (my bar: under 1 s with playback continuing and no silent gap); if it exceeds 1 s, the spec's "Preparing audio" status becomes necessary and that is a 09a gap. Spec States updated.
   - *No haptic at a fade's limit:* accepted and removed from the spec. The thumb and readout stop, which is visible. Polish for later: when the other fade leaves less than 5 s, the rail still looks full; revisit if people drag against the stop.
   - *Bars follow Volume on release:* fine, and better than the spec: the panel covers the timeline while a slider moves, so nobody can see the bars change live. The spec line is corrected. A side effect worth knowing: people set a fade without seeing its shape; they see it when Done closes the panel. Not a defect; a candidate for a later mini fade preview.
5. **Fades are linear:** accepted. Linear is the plain default (Audacity's, most editors'), and the timeline bars use the same linear curve, so what is drawn is what is heard. Guidance, not tested: a short linear fade-out can sound abrupt at the very end. If the owner's ear check says so, change the curve in one place (`FadeGains` and `fadeGain` in the painter together, so picture and sound still agree).

## Waveforms
- **Tokens:** pass. `MemixColors.onTrack` at `MemixSize.waveformClipOpacity` (0.6); bar width, gap and inset from tokens; the label's patch is the clip's color. I recomputed the contrast: bars on `track-audio` 3.29:1 and on `track-meme-sound` 3.59:1 (the spec's 3.3 and 3.6), both over the 3:1 needed for a graphic (SC 1.4.11). QA still measures one frame.
- **Seen:** bars centered, minimum-width bars read as a dotted line for silence (`waveform-audio`), selected clip keeps its outline and handles with bars unchanged (`waveform-meme-sound-selected`). Fade-in over 120 ms has a frame ticker (`FadeInWaveforms`); no loop. Hidden from semantics; "Audio effects on" is added to the clip's state by code (`TimelineSemantics.kt`).
- **Polish 1, bar stubs around the label (every clip with a label):** a bar that is partly behind the patch shows only its top and bottom ends as small dashes above and below the patch (`waveform-meme-sound`, "Boom" and "Bruh"; `waveform-audio`, both labels). It reads as noise at the most visible spot. **Fix:** skip any bar whose x falls inside the patch's width, so the waveform is cleanly interrupted by the label.
- **Polish 2, the patch over the wash:** in `waveform-meme-sound` the "Boom" label's patch is bright blue across the part of the clip past the end of the video, so the wash looks pierced (spec: the wash covers bars and everything). `drawItemLabel` runs after `drawPastEndWash`. **Fix:** paint the patch under the wash (or clip the patch to the part before the video's end).
- **Not rendered, for QA:** a clip with fades (bars shaped), a voiceover clip, zoomed far in on a 32-value catalog waveform (stepped bars, accepted in the spec), a 10-minute clip at the widest zoom (draw cost; mobile-performance check).

## Copy and budgets, five languages
Pass except `fx_seconds` hi (must-fix 2). All 09a keys are present in en, id, es, pt, hi and equal the spec text; placeholders map to `%1$s`. Budget by eye: the longest strings (`fx_reset_a11y` es about 38 of 44, `fx_fade_too_short` es about 47 of 44, which is the spec's own mismatch: its budget column said 44 for a string that was already longer; the string is a toast and wraps to two lines, so no change) fit their slots. Visible-label-first names hold (WCAG 2.5.3): "Reset all audio effects", "Restablecer todos…", "Redefinir todos…", "सभी ऑडियो इफ़ेक्ट रीसेट करें" all contain the visible Reset word. Decimal marks: comma for id, es, pt by `formatTenths`, point for en and hi, as specced. Minus sign is a real minus (U+2212).

## 200% font
- `panel-hi-200` and `panel-es-200`: title and Reset/Done wrap as in P2-04; the tab row has moved into the scrolling content (the 40% rule worked), underline and dot intact, no clipped matras, Devanagari line height holds. **But nothing of the content is visible at 360 x 640 at 200%**: header and tabs fill the panel, and no slider peeks.
