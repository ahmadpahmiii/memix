## Matches the spec
- **Layout:**
  - The status bar inset shows `canvas`.
  - The top bar is 56 dp: `space-1` above and below a 48 dp Close. It holds Close only (the chips and Export wait for P1-11 and P1-12).
  - The stage is `stage` black, with the canvas frame inset `space-2` and centered.
  - The transport row is at least `touch-target` tall and has the `hairline` divider along its bottom edge.
  - The 60/40 split is computed from the window only, the timeline minimum is a named stand-in (R6), and the preview never resizes with content.
  - The tool bar region is `toolbar-height` of `surface`, plus the navigation inset.
  - The play button sits on the center line, between two equal slots.
- **States:**
  - Every state in the table is reachable: opening (static `surface-raised` frame, no spinner), default paused, playing, at end (Play restarts from 0:00 within one frame), preview error with Try again, missing media (toast once per open), save failing (both banner texts), leaving while a save fails (sheet), and the empty draft.
  - After a successful save the banner and the sheet go away without a message, and "Changes saved" is spoken.
  - `firstFrameShown` is never reset by an edit, so the gray opening frame can't flash over the preview after each change.
- **Playback:**
  - Play is optimistic. The end stops without looping.
  - Playback pauses for app background, undo, redo, the test edit, opening the sheet, audio focus loss (and stays paused after a call) and `ACTION_AUDIO_BECOMING_NOISY`.
- **Undo/redo (P1-07):**
  - Both sit in the right slot. Disabled shows `text-muted` and taps do nothing (`clickable(enabled = false)`, so TalkBack says "disabled").
  - "Undo: {edit}" and "Redo: {edit}" toasts appear. `tool_use` fires only when the project changes.
- **Saving:**
  - Saving is silent. The banner has Free up space (StorageFull) or no action (other errors), plus ×. × hides it until a save succeeds and a later one fails again.
  - Retries happen on the next change and on return to the foreground.
  - The sheet: Free up space is primary and Leave anyway is secondary. Close, the scrim and back keep the user in the editor.
- **Tokens only:** I found no color, dp, sp, font or shape literal in the new UI. The exceptions are `TimelineMinHeight` (layout math, R6) and the toast timings (named constants). No v1 values.
- **Copy:**
  - The English matches the spec word for word, and `editor_free_up_space` reuses `import_free_up_space` as the spec asks.
  - Every key exists in id, es, pt and hi, and all are within budget. The tightest are pt `editor_unsaved_title` 40/40, id `editor_missing_media` 89/90 and pt `editor_save_failed` 91/100.
  - Hindi puts the total first in `a11y_time_of`.
  - P1-02's retired keys are gone.
- **Accessibility:**
  - Order: Close → banner (traversal index −1 inside the stage group) → Preview ("Preview, 9:16", with click label Play or Pause) → timecode (one node, "3.2 seconds of 8 seconds", not live) → Play/Pause → Undo → Redo.
  - The toast is a polite live region and never takes focus. Timeouts go through `calculateRecommendedTimeoutMillis` (2 s base for what happened, 4 s for explanations).
  - The sheet takes focus and uses its title as the pane title.
  - Every control is 48 dp or more.
- **Motion:**
  - The editor push takes 320 ms (120 ms fade with reduce motion).
  - The Play/Pause icon cross-fades over 120 ms.
  - The toast and banner fade over 120 ms.
  - The sheet slides over 200 ms, or fades over 120 ms with reduce motion.
  - Undo applies instantly.
- **Icons:** `ic_undo` follows the grammar: a solid arrowhead as the core, a 2 dp butt-capped stroke and mitred joins.

## Must fix before end-of-phase QA (spec errors; small)
1. **Toast text is cut off.** `Toast.kt:66-71` (`maxLines = MAX_LINES`, `:96`) with `Text`'s default ellipsis. The spec said "up to 3 lines". That is wrong on two counts:
   - **The save banner shares its row with Free up space and ×.** At 360 dp that leaves the message about 140 dp, roughly 20 characters a line at `label`. That's 3 lines in English, but the pt (71 characters), es (69) and hi messages need 4 lines, so their end is cut at 100% font. This is the one message about losing work.
   - **At 200% font, any toast over about 60 characters loses its end** ("A clip's file is missing…"). That fails WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.4 (Resize text: no loss of content).

   **Fix:**
   - No line cap: the copy budgets keep toasts short.
   - Persistent variant: the message and × share the first row, and the Quiet action goes on its own row below, aligned to the end. This is the same move as Compose Material 3's `Snackbar(actionOnNewLine = true)`, which Google recommends for long action text.

   Spec lines broken: P1-04 Accessibility "Toast text wraps up to 3 lines" and Toast README "up to 3 lines" (both corrected).
2. **The Try again button is invisible.** `VideoEditorScreen.kt:259-265`: the error message sits on a `surface-raised` frame (`:261`), and Try again is a Secondary button (`:263`), whose fill is also `surface-raised`. The button's shape disappears and it reads as a plain line of text.

   **Fix:** the error frame uses `surface`. Secondary on `surface` is the normal one-step value change. The text stays `text-secondary` (5.7:1 or more on `surface`).

   Spec line broken: P1-04 States → "Preview error" (corrected: frame in `surface`).

## Polish
3. **Timecode wrapping.** `VideoEditorScreen.kt:425-431` and `:474`. The length is a separate FlowRow item, "/ 00:08.00", with a `space-2` gap before it.
   - **At 360 dp and 100% font:** 18 Space Mono characters at 12 sp (about 7.3 dp each) plus the 8 dp gap come to about 140 dp. The slot has 140 dp (156 minus the `space-4` gutter). So English may already wrap in `default-paused`.
   - **At 200%:** "/ 00:08.00" no longer fits on one line, so it breaks into "/" and "00:08.00". The result is three lines with an orphaned slash, where the spec asks for two.

   **Fix:**
   - On one line, draw a single text "00:03.20 / 00:08.00" with real spaces (19 characters, about 139 dp).
   - When that doesn't fit, show the time over the length with no slash. The `text` and `text-muted` colors already tell them apart.

   Check on `default-paused` and `timecode-hi-200`. Spec "Large font or hour-long projects" is updated to say this.
4. **Undo feedback while the save banner shows.** I confirm R4's design: the banner wins and toasts never stack. But the build doesn't make toasts wait; they time out unseen (`VideoEditorScreen.kt:296-303`), and that's the right call, because a queued "Undo: Trim" shown 30 s later would be stale. What's missing: TalkBack users then hear nothing after Undo, which breaks spec Accessibility ("After undo or redo, the toast text is announced politely"). **Fix:** while the banner is up, speak the toast text through a polite live region only, using the same pattern as `SavedAgainAnnouncement`.
5. **Play does nothing in the empty and preview-error states.** `VideoEditorViewModel.kt:131-133` returns early, while the button at `VideoEditorScreen.kt:389-393` looks enabled: a dead control. **Fix:** pass `enabled = false` in `StageContent.EMPTY` and `StageContent.ERROR`. The spec States now say this.
6. **The ×'s press is invisible on the banner.** `IconButton.kt:45` presses to `surface-raised`, which is the toast's own fill. **Fix:** an IconButton on `surface-raised` presses to `hairline`, one step lighter, per DESIGN_SYSTEM States. A parameter is fine. The rule is in the new IconButton README.
7. **`editor_dismiss` translations:**
   - **id:** "Tutup pesan" ("close message"): approved. It is clearer than "Abaikan" ("ignore"), which could read as ignoring the problem.
   - **es:** "Descartar" means "discard". Spoken next to "your changes aren't saved", it can be heard as "discard the changes". Change it to "Cerrar aviso".
   - **pt and hi:** for the same reason, use the same pattern: pt "Fechar aviso" (now "Dispensar"), hi "संदेश बंद करें" (now "खारिज करें").

   These are machine drafts and P5-06 has the final say. The spec translator note now says "means hide this message; never a word for discard or reject".

   Files: `values-es/strings.xml:51`, `values-pt/strings.xml:51`, `values-hi/strings.xml:51`.
8. **Free up space when no storage screen can open (R7).** Today the button stays and does nothing if the launch fails (some OEM builds; always on iOS until P7). **Fix:** hide the banner's action and the sheet's Free up space button when the storage screen can't be opened. That's the same rule as P1-02's `not-enough-space-no-button`. The sheet then shows only Leave anyway, and its close button, the scrim and back still keep the user in the editor. The texts still say what's wrong. Spec Saving updated.
9. **Translator pointer.** Line 3 of the id, es, pt and hi `strings.xml` files points only to the P1-02 spec. Add the P1-04 pointer, as in the English file. Low priority.

## Answers to the engineer's design questions
- **R4 (no stacking while the banner shows):** confirmed. One message slot at the top of the stage, the banner first. Toasts that arrive under it are dropped, not queued, and spoken (polish 4). Added to spec → Saving.
- **IconButton and Toast/FadingToast:** both belong in the design system.
  - IconButton now has a component spec, `design/system/components/IconButton/README.md` (shape, states, the pressed rule on `surface-raised`, the disabled rule).
  - The Toast README now covers FadingToast, the no-line-cap rule and the persistent layout.
  - `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` → Compose notes lists IconButton.
  - Later, not blocking: Sheet's private `CloseButton` (`Sheet.kt:225-241`, round press shape) can become an IconButton, so every no-fill icon control presses the same way (`radius-md`).
- **The empty tool-bar region in `surface` until P1-06:** approved. It keeps the 60/40 split and every P1-04 screenshot at their final proportions, it's not a control (so it's not a dead control), and users never see it: P1-06 ships in the same phase.
- **`editor_dismiss` id "Tutup pesan":** approved (see polish 7).
- **R5 (Escape works once something has focus):** accepted for P1. A hardware-keyboard user presses Tab first.
- **R9 (focus back after a sheet):** stays a follow-up for QA's keyboard pass. One fact that may remove the worry: Compose's `clickable` is focusable only in non-touch (keyboard) input mode, so a `requestFocus` on Close after the sheet closes should do nothing for touch users and draw the ring only for keyboard users. Check this against the current foundation docs.
- **R1 (a full re-prepare on every edit):** design note for P1-06. After an edit, the preview must keep showing the last frame until the new one is ready, with no black flash. Add "edit, then look at the preview" to the P1-06 hand check and screen recording.
- **R3:** the copy for an unreadable draft belongs in the P1-14 spec. I'll cover it there.
- **R10:** the three size tokens are already in `design/tokens.json` and DESIGN_SYSTEM. The Kotlin properties land with P1-05.

## Spec corrected
`docs/ux/specs/P1-04-editor-and-preview.md`:
- **Preview error:** the frame is `surface`, and Play is disabled.
- **Empty draft:** Play is disabled.
- **Timecode:** when wrapped, the length shows alone under the time, with no slash.
