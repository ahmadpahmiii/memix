# Design review: P3-05 Sound picker sheet (video editor)

Reviewed 11 Nov 2026 against `docs/ux/specs/P3-05-sound-picker-sheet.md`. Evidence: 17 desktop renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P3-05/` (360 dp wide), the engineer's handoff, `SoundPickerSheet.kt`, `SoundDetailsSheet.kt`, `Sheet.kt`. Landing-at-end and overhang are not rendered; they are checked at phase-end QA on the emulator.

## Verdict: Pass with polish

No must-fix blocks the commit. Five polish items, two of them small code changes (items 1 and 2). The spec was wrong in two places (height, longer-than-video); both are corrected in the spec.

## What passes
- **Tokens only:** `MemixSize.soundAddSize` is wired in SoundRow and exists in `tokens.json`. No raw dp, sp or Color in `feature/sounds`. No v1 colors. The one blue action per row is the Add block; selection white (tab underline); the playing band is `surface-raised`. No outlines, no gradients.
- **Two-tap path:** `picker-open` (360 × 800) and `picker-open-640` both show three rows with their Add blocks without scrolling, search or tab change. Nothing selected: tool, Add. Clip selected: pill, Add. Passes the Done when. Counted by hand on a phone at QA.
- **States that can exist now:** default, playing (blue disc, blue waveform, `surface-raised` band), search result, offline cached (strip, rows normal), offline starter, downloading (cancel disc, Downloading..., rail, Add in disabled look), failed (Retry disc, `danger` meta, Add stays active so a tap retries), storage full (strip with Free up space, Add disabled), details pane (back arrow, heart, close, waveform, facts, pinned Add to video), empty My sounds, Spanish, Hindi at 200%. All match the spec.
- **Targets:** disc, heart, Add, close, back are 48 dp in the renders; the strip's Quiet button is large enough.
- **Copy and budgets:** `picker_overhang_toast`, `picker_back_a11y`, `sound_detail_add_to_video` exist in all five languages and match the spec table. Longest: Portuguese overhang toast at 105 characters, under the 110 budget. `sound_detail_add_to_video` longest is Indonesian at 19, under 28. `picker_back_a11y` longest is Indonesian at 23, under 30. Wording is the spec's machine draft; the final say is P5-06.
- **Motion:** pane swap is a Crossfade at `durationSheet` (200 ms) and `durationPress` (120 ms) with reduce motion; the Sheet's grow uses `snap()` with reduce motion. Matches the Motion table.
- **TalkBack (from code and spec; verify on device at QA):** pane title is the sheet title; the details pane title changes to the sound's name and is announced; back button has its own label; `coveredByModal` is kept. Facts merge into one stop each.
- **Hindi at 200%:** Devanagari names and matras are not clipped, the meta wraps to as many lines as it needs (never cut), tabs scroll, the search field has moved into the list below the tabs, the header no longer eats the sheet.

## Polish (do now or at the next touch; none blocks)
1. **Sound longer than the whole video** (code change, one condition). My table row was wrong; see answer 2. Land at the playhead always, as every other case; keep the 0.5 s rule and the toast. Today a 9.9 s sound lands where you tapped, and a 10.1 s one jumps to 0:00. Spec line: "Where it lands" table, last row.
2. **Landing copy failure, phone full** (small). Show the existing `sound_dl_storage_toast` instead of `explain_sound_unreadable` when the copy fails for lack of space. No new key. Spec: "Download fails mid-add".
3. **The sheet shrinks below its opening height when the list is short** (`picker-search.png`: one result, the sheet is about 300 dp and its top edge sits 200 dp lower than in `picker-open`). `Sheet.kt` only sets a maximum (`heightIn(max = ...)`), never a minimum, so the tabs move under the thumb after a search or on a short Favorites list. With search focus the sheet grows at once, so the worst case is rarer on a device, but tab switches still jump. Give the list area a minimum so the sheet keeps `minOpeningHeight` while open (for example `heightIn(min = minOpeningHeight)` while not expanded). Spec: "Height" (added sentence) and Sheet README.
4. **Search placeholder is cut at 200% font** (`picker-hi-200`: "bruh, boom या sad..."). A placeholder may wrap to two lines or shrink its wording; low priority because the field is a hint only, but check that the field's accessible name stays "Search sounds".
5. **Details pane at 436 dp** shows four of the five facts; the fifth ("On this phone") sits half under the fold above the pinned button (`details-pane.png`). It scrolls and the button stays pinned (P3-03's rule), so this is correct, but the clipped label reads like a bug. Add a little bottom content padding or let the scroll area end with a fade-free gap so the cut falls between facts. QA: confirm the scroll works with TalkBack.
- The render artifacts (a row cut by the harness frame, an English "Meme sounds" title under the Hindi render) are harness effects, not defects; confirm the title is localized on the emulator.

## Answers to the six questions
1. **364 dp was wrong; 436 dp is right.** I counted 48 dp rows; a row is about 70 dp (48 target, 16 padding, 8 gap) and the renders show it. Updated in the spec ("Height") and the Sheet README. On a 640 dp phone the sheet takes about 68% of the window and leaves a 204 dp strip of preview; I accept that, because a row in reach beats a bigger preview (principle 2: sound first).
2. **No, not what I meant.** The table row's reason ("the playhead would put the start before 0:00") was false. The playhead rule wins in every other row because the user chose that moment; a special case at "longer than the whole video" makes a 10.1 s sound land at 0:00 and a 9.9 s one at the playhead. Corrected: playhead always, overhang wash plus toast when more than 0.5 s is unheard. For your example (14 s sound at 3 s of a 10 s video) it lands at 3 s with 7 s unheard, the toast says trim or move earlier. Needs the one-condition change in `SoundEdits`. If you prefer not to touch it in this ticket, log it as a known deviation; it is rare.
3. **Yes, a space-specific line, but reuse `sound_dl_storage_toast`** ("Your phone is full, so this sound can't be saved.", no action). No new string. A missing file keeps "That sound won't play. Try another one."
4. **Accepted.** A fixed font scale of 1.5 is simpler and safe: at 1.5 and 2.0 the header never exceeds 40% anyway, so it hides the search slightly early, which costs one scroll at most. Spec, Sheet README, `tokens.json` and DESIGN_SYSTEM now say so, and `pinned-bar-max-fraction` no longer lists the picker. Keep the focus order as drawn (title, close, tabs, search, rows) at that scale.
5. **Accepted.** Draw nothing; a landing from the phone is a frame or two, and a disabled flash would be noise. Mark the block disabled for TalkBack while it runs. A landing over 300 ms (a long import) shows the disabled Add look. Spec: "Other states".
6. **Confirmed.** List and details panes now; Report and Import come with P3-10 and P3-07. Back order (keyboard, pane, sheet) is wired through `onBack`. The known title-lookup fix for a reopened landed library sound is the right fix; verify the label after reopening a draft at QA.

## Token changes
None to values. Doc-only: `pinned-bar-max-fraction` usage line (`design/tokens.json`) and the DESIGN_SYSTEM row no longer name the picker; logged in `docs/ux/CHANGELOG.md` (sync to design canvas). No Kotlin handoff.
