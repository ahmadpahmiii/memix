# Design review: P2-04 part a (Crop screen; crop, rotate, flip, straighten)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the six renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P2-04/` (desktop, stand-in picture; the Edges panel and the touch handles aren't in them), the geometry references in `docs/qa/references/P2-04-image-tools/`, the spec and the handoff. The Edit photo panel, adjustments, filters and deep-fry are 04b.

## Verdict: Approved with polish (one must-fix)

## What works
- **Layout** matches the spec: Close, title, Reset (Quiet) and Done (the one blue action) on top; the picture on the black stage with the two-tone frame, corner L handles and edge bars; the aspect tiles (Free selected in white; Original, Canvas and ratios drawn as gray shapes, scrolling sideways); the four tool icons plus the Edges Secondary button; Straighten with its degree readout.
- **Straighten** (`80-crop-screen-edited`, `geometry-straighten-10`): the picture is turned and scaled up so no corner is empty; the outside of the frame is dimmed by the scrim; at 10° the scale reads about 1.2× as specified.
- **A crop in the editor** (`80-editor-cropped-1x1`): the layer is the crop's shape, fitted inside the canvas, the canvas unchanged (not a canvas change), "Crop" in the layer bar.
- **References:** each geometry step exists at 1080 and 2160 (rotate, flip, straighten both ways, crop, the combined order).
- **200% font** (es, hi): the bottom area wraps and scrolls, the aspect row scrolls, Edges wraps to its own row, nothing is cut. In Spanish the top bar goes to two rows (title on the first, Reset and Done on the second); Done stays the primary and the reading order is intact. Accepted.

## Must fix
- **M1. Done sits against the top edge.** In `80-crop-screen-opened` (and 360 × 640 and Hindi) the Done button runs from the very top of the bar, its top corners flat, while Close and the title are centered in the bar: the row is top-aligned in a 56 dp bar, not centered. Spec: top bar 56 dp with its controls centered (the Layout item 1 and the editor top bars before it). Fix: center the row vertically (Done 48 dp with 4 dp above and below). In Spanish, where it wraps to two rows, it already looks right.

## Polish
- **P1. Straighten's fill starts at the left end.** At 0° the rail is half blue, and at −10° it is mostly empty, which reads as "turned a lot" or "not at all". A bipolar slider fills from its zero. Rule added to `design/system/components/Slider/README.md` (optional `origin`: fill from the origin to the thumb, a short tick marks the origin); use it for Straighten now and for the Adjust sliders in 04b (brightness, contrast and the others run from negative to positive).
- **P2. Edges: a held press applies only on release (known limit).** Nothing on this screen is an undo step until Done, so the frame can follow each repeat live. Please do: a user holding Plus should see the frame grow, not wait. Same readout update each step.
- **P3. Rotate and flip are instant (known limit).** The spec has a 200 ms turn or mirror (instant with reduce motion). Accept for now; the picture and the frame jump together, which is readable. Add the 200 ms turn when time allows, since a 90° jump on a tall picture can disorient.
- **P4. The frame has no arrow-key control (known limit).** Accepted: keyboard shortcuts are Later (L-01), and the Edges panel covers keyboard and single-pointer use. I amended the spec's keyboard line to say so.
- **P5. No render of the Edges panel.** It is the single-pointer alternative to dragging (SC 2.5.1 and 2.5.7), so it needs a look: four rows (Left, Right, Top, Bottom) with minus, plus and a percent readout, on the bottom area, plus Done. Device or render check at QA, at 360 × 640 and in Hindi 200%.
- **P6. The top bar wraps at es and hi 200% (known limit).** Accepted as seen in the Spanish render; Hindi stays on one row.

## Part a, device pass (QA)
- Drag a corner, an edge and the frame itself; the 10% minimum (the drag just stops); the thirds grid only while dragging; handles at the stage edge keep 48 dp targets.
- Aspect tile on a rotated and flipped picture: the largest centered frame of that shape; Free unlocks; Canvas follows the canvas ratio.
- Rotate then flip then straighten: the order is fixed and Reset returns to the state the screen opened in; Close, back and Escape discard; Done is one undo step "Crop".
- Reopen the screen on an edited layer: the crop, turn and straighten are the stored values, adjustable, not only replaceable.
- Under a picture-area format, an as-placed photo is placed again for its new shape; a moved photo stays.
- TalkBack: the frame is hidden from it; each rotate and flip announces its result ("Rotated left"); Straighten steps by 1°.
- Preview, render hook and export equal for a combined edit (the references are the geometry; compare at 1080 and 2160).

# Part b (Edit photo: Filters, Adjust, Deep-fry)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 17 renders `90-*` (desktop, stand-in picture), the contact sheets in `docs/qa/references/P2-04-image-tools/` (filters at 100% and 50%, adjustments, deep-fry, on a chart photo and a grid photo, at 1080 and 2160), the spec and the handoff. Nothing ran on a device.

## Verdict: Changes requested (two must-fixes)

## Do the 20 filters look like their names?
**None is broken:** ramps are smooth with no banding, whites and blacks hold, skin-tone patches stay plausible, and 50% is a clean mix. **Distinct and usable, not yet.** Read on the contact sheets at tile size (72 dp), the strong looks are clear: Vivid, Mono, Noir, Fade, Vintage, Sepia, Pastel, Punch, Drama, Bleach. But eight sit within a hair of None or of each other, and three pairs read as one:
- **Film, Cinema, Chrome, Aqua, Dusk, Cool** are all faint cool-ish tints; on the blue grid photo they are nearly identical to None and to each other. **Neon** and **Sunset** are faint too (only the white highlights move).
- **Fade, Matte and Pastel** are the same washed-out look with small differences.
- **Warm** barely warms.

A strip where a third of the tiles look the same is a strip people stop scrolling. My recipe numbers were starting values ("the reference renders pin them"); the renders show they were too timid.

## Must fix
- **M1. Make the weak filters visible and distinct.** New recipes are in the spec table (rows marked "review 04b"): Warm and Cool stronger; Film and Cinema stronger and different from each other (Film lifts and desaturates with teal shadows and warm highlights, Cinema is the harder teal-and-orange split); Pastel brighter and slightly pink; Matte dulls the highlights; Sunset, Neon, Aqua and Chrome stronger; Dusk turns purple so it is not a second Cool. Regenerate the 20 `.lut` files, the contact sheets and the strip frames; I re-check the sheets. **Pass test:** on both test photos each filter's mean color shift from None is at least about 8/255, no two filters' mean shifts are within about 4/255 of each other (the script can print the numbers), and a viewer can tell any two tiles apart at 72 dp. Names and ids don't change. This is a look change before anything ships; after the closed test (about 25 Jan 2027) it would need a schema bump.
- **M2. Tab labels are cut with an ellipsis at 200%** (`90-edit-photo-*-spanish-200`: "Filtr…", "Ajus…", "Dee…"; Hindi "डीप-…"). Breaks the spec's 200% rule (tab labels wrap, nothing cut). Three equal tabs can't hold these words at 200% on 360 dp. **Fix (SegmentedTabs rule):** at large font the tabs size to their labels and the row scrolls sideways, each label whole on one line (a word is never cut), the selected tab scrolled into view.

## Polish
- **P1. Deep-fry and resolution.** Deep-fry looks right (crunchy, hot saturation, a warm-red cast; 25% is a hint, 60% is the meme, 100% is extreme but not broken). The principal may make grain and sharpen resolution-independent; I support that, it is what the spec intends (cells 0.002 of the shorter side, radius 0.0015). Measured: only 77.5% of pixels agree within 3/255 between 1080 and 2160 because cells and radius round to whole pixels. Any approach that makes the 2160 frame equal the 1080 frame scaled by 2 (97% or better within the current 12/255) is fine: fractional cells from normalized coordinates and a sharpen sigma in the same units. Re-render the references after.
- **P2. Adjust and Intensity under the 40% cap.** At 360 × 640 the Intensity row is below the fold when a filter is on, and Adjust shows two rows. The panel scrolls; QA notes whether testers find Intensity. If not, put Intensity directly under the tabs, above the strip.
- **P3. The header at 200%** takes two or three rows (title, Reset and Done, tabs), so the controls start low. Confirm on the device that Reset and Done stay pinned while the content scrolls and the content is reachable.
- **P4. Bipolar sliders** (Adjust) use the new origin fill and tick as specified: good (`90-edit-photo-adjust`).
- **P5. Known gaps accepted:** no keyboard navigation in the filter strip (keyboard shortcuts are Later, L-01; TalkBack reaches the tiles as a single-choice group: device check); no iOS "unsupported" explanation yet (P7).

## For the device pass (QA)
- A real portrait photo with a face through all 20 filters at 100% and 50%: skin stays natural in the mild ones.
- Strip thumbnails come in order with no spinner, and the selected one scrolls into view after Undo.
- Sliders live while dragging (drawn at a lower size) and the committed frame equals the final export; one undo step per release; Reset on each tab is one step.
- Preview, render hook and export equal for a combined edit (the `scene-all-tools` references); speed of the CPU pass on the owner's phone (the pre-agreed must-fix is a GL pass on the same LUT data if it janks).
- Hindi and Spanish at 200%: tabs scroll with whole labels (M2).

## Fix check, part b (7 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** On the new sheets (`filters-100-chart-1080`, `filters-100-base-1080`) every filter is now readable against None and against its neighbours: Warm golden, Cool blue, Aqua cyan-green, Dusk purple, Sunset pink-violet, Neon magenta highlights, Chrome hard and silver, Matte dull and flat, Pastel light and pink, Cinema navy shadows with warm highlights. Fade, Matte and Pastel are now three different looks. The pass test numbers (10.3/255 or more, no pair within 4/255, closest Mono and Noir at 7.0) are accepted.
- **Film at 7.7/255: accepted.** The mean is taken over the whole color lattice, and Film's change is concentrated where it should be (teal shadows, lifted and slightly desaturated, warm highlights); on the sheet its shadow patch is plainly teal against None and against Cinema's navy. It is the intended "subtle" look, and it is not a duplicate of any neighbour. No change.
- **M2 fixed.** `90-edit-photo-filters-none-spanish-200` shows "Filtros", "Ajustar" and "Deep-" in tabs sized to their labels, the row scrolling sideways (the third is cut at the track edge as the scroll cue; each label is whole when scrolled to). Device check: scrolling the selected tab into view.
- **Tile:** now `MemixTheme.size.filterTileSize` (`filter-tile-size`, 72 dp); noted for the audit.

## Final verdict, part b: Approved with polish
P1 to P5 above stand (resolution-independent grain and sharpen are in; Intensity under the cap, the 200% header and the device checks go to QA).
