## Matches the spec
- **Layout:**
  - Full width on `canvas`, forced left-to-right, no track-label column.
  - The ruler is pinned and the lanes scroll vertically under it.
  - Track order uses a stable sort, so lanes of one kind keep model order. Empty tracks are left out.
  - Row sizes: main row 48 with a 40 clip; other rows 32 with 28 items.
  - The ruler is the `timecode` line plus `space-1` above and below (24 dp), and it grows at 200%.
- **Ruler:**
  - Labels are `timecode` in `text-muted`. Major ticks are `space-2` tall and minor ticks half that; minor ticks hide below `space-2` spacing.
  - Nothing is drawn before 0:00.
  - The step comes from the measured widest label plus `space-4`. It matches the spec table at 480, 160, 40, 10 and 2 dp per second (engineer's harness; renders zoom-max, open, whole-video). At 200% it picks 5 s by itself (render hindi-200).
- **Zoom:**
  - Range: default 40, max 480, and the "whole video in half the width" minimum. The menu steps ×2 and ÷2.
  - Pinch keeps the playhead time. The scale snaps back into range when the video gets shorter.
  - Menu steps animate over 200 ms and jump with reduce motion. The item at its limit is `text-muted` and inert (render zoom-max).
- **Items and lanes:**
  - `surface` bands run from 0:00 to the end. Items use `radius-sm`, start exactly on time, end `space-1` short, and are never thinner than `playhead-width` (render whole-video).
  - Labels are `caption` in `on-track`. They stick to the visible left edge with an ellipsis (render drag-scroll).
  - Past the end: a `scrim` wash, and the label switches to `text` exactly at the end line (renders sound-selected-near-end, at-end).
  - Missing file shows `track-video` and "File missing" in `text` (render missing-file).
  - Tiles show `track-video` until loaded, fade in over 120 ms, use the nearest frame as a stand-in while zooming, and stay `track-video` when a frame fails. No spinner, no shimmer.
- **Playhead:**
  - A white 2 dp line with a 12 dp head, from the top of the ruler to the bottom of the visible lanes.
  - It sits above items and below the menu (render zoom-menu), and never moves on screen.
- **Gestures:**
  - Touch-down pauses playback. Drag scrubs; a fling stops exactly at 0:00 and at the end, with no overscroll.
  - A ruler tap seeks over 200 ms and keeps the selection (render ruler-tap). A ruler long-press opens the menu.
  - A tap on an item selects it; a tap on the selected item does nothing. A tap on an empty lane, the leading area or below the lanes clears the selection.
  - No snapping while scrubbing. Axis lock comes from the two scrollables' touch slop.
- **Selection:**
  - The `stroke-selection` outline sits outside the item with a `stroke-hairline` gap and concentric corners.
  - Handles follow P1-06 → Trim: `trim-handle-width`, outer corners `radius-sm`, an `on-primary` grip at half height, as tall as the outline.
  - The badge is `timecode` on `scrim` and shows only when there's room.
  - The selection is kept through scroll, zoom, playback and undo, and fades in over 120 ms.
- **States:** one clip, many clips, many tracks, at end, selected, past end, hour-long, empty, missing file and Hindi at 200% all show as specified in the renders.
- **Deferred correctly, no dead controls:** the original-audio toggle, the "Add a meme sound" row and Add media aren't drawn. Handles are drawn but drag only with P1-06. Approved, same rule as P1-04.
- **Tokens only:**
  - The audit finds nothing in `:feature:video-editor`: no `Color(0x…)`, dp, sp, `FontFamily`, `RoundedCornerShape`, `Brush`/gradient, `border` or v1 hex.
  - The layout numbers live in `TimelineMath.kt` with a pointer to the spec (request 4).
  - `MemixSize.playheadHeadSize`, `trimHandleWidth` and `iconSmall` match `design/tokens.json` (request 1).
- **v2 rules:**
  - Blue appears only on meme sounds. White appears only on selection, handles and the playhead. Track colors are as tokenized.
  - No outlines around shapes (the selection stroke is the selection) and no gradients. The only shadow is the menu's `shadow-float`.
- **Menu component** matches its README:
  - Look: `surface-raised`, `radius-md`, `shadow-float`. Rows are 48 dp, `body`, with `space-4` padding; the width is at least 3 × 48.
  - States: pressed to `hairline`; disabled in `text-muted`.
  - Placement: above the press point, else below, `space-2` inside the window.
  - Behavior: closes on an outside tap, back or Escape; 120 ms fade; zoom steps keep it open.
- **Copy:**
  - The English matches the spec word for word. All 24 keys exist in id, es, pt and hi, within budget.
  - The tightest are id `timeline_zoom_fit` 23/24, es `a11y_state_after_end` 52/56 and es `timeline_a11y` 15/16.
  - Hindi reorders the placeholders in `a11y_clip_media`. Spoken strings hold no timecode digits.
- **Accessibility:**
  - The container is "Timeline", with the three zoom actions.
  - The playhead is adjustable by one frame and has four jump actions. Its value comes from `a11y_time_of` and is re-spoken at most ten times a second.
  - Items read `a11y_clip_media` / `a11y_item` plus their states. Double-tap selects an item and brings its start under the playhead.
  - The 28 dp items in 32 dp rows are the documented exception.
- **Smoothness:** the engineer measured that a 60-step fling recomposes nothing of the timeline. That supports "no visible lag"; the phone numbers come at QA.

## Must fix before end-of-phase QA
**M1. A missing file is silent for TalkBack.**
- **Where:** `TimelineSemantics.kt:278-290` (`itemStates`) adds only muted, sound detached and past the end, and `TrackSemantics` (`:161-170`) never receives the missing sources.
- **The problem:** a sighted user sees "File missing" on the clip. A TalkBack user hears "Video, clip 2 of 3, 6 seconds long, starts at 3 seconds", the same as any other clip. P1-04's toast then says "A clip's file is missing, so it plays black. Delete that clip to fix it." A screen-reader user is told to delete a clip they can't find, which is a dead end.
- **Fix:**
  - Pass `thumbnails?.missingSources.orEmpty()` from `Timeline.kt:203` into `TrackSemantics` and on into `ItemFacts`.
  - In `itemStates`, for a main clip whose `media` is in that set, put `stringResource(Res.string.clip_missing_file)` first.
  - No new string.
- **Spec lines:** States → "Missing file" and Accessibility → TalkBack structure 4 ("States are appended"). My spec left this state out; both are corrected (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.3.1, Guidance).

## Polish
**P1. The ruler label inset is approved as built.** Labels start `space-1` right of their tick, so text never touches the tick line. The spec's "starts at its tick" is updated.

**P2. The zoom menu gets its own pane title** (question 2).
- **Where:** `Timeline.kt:360`.
- **Change:** use a new string `timeline_zoom_menu` = "Zoom" (budget 16) instead of `timeline_a11y`.
- **Drafts:** id "Zoom", es "Zoom", pt "Zoom", hi "ज़ूम".

**P3. Unnamed items get their own phrasing** (question 3).
- **Where:** `TimelineSemantics.kt:268-275`. When the label is `ItemLabel.None`, today it speaks the track name twice.
- **Change:** use the new `a11y_item_unnamed`; exact copy under question 3.

**P4. A LongPress haptic when the zoom menu opens.**
- **Where:** `Timeline.kt:163-167` (`onLongPress`).
- **Change:** call `LocalHapticFeedback.current.performHapticFeedback(HapticFeedbackType.LongPress)` when the menu opens. Check the name against the current Compose docs.
- **Why:** Android uses `LONG_PRESS` for "a long press that triggers an action" (Guidance, Android haptics principles), and P1-06 already uses it for the long-press lift. Without it, the long-press gives no sign it worked until the menu fades in.
- **Spec:** P1-05 Zoom and P1-06's haptics table are updated.

**P5. TalkBack reading order across lanes: confirm on the device, with a fallback ready.**
- **What's built:** nodes for the items on screen, and each row is scrollable for TalkBack. That's the lazy-list pattern. Compose leaves off-screen nodes out of the accessibility tree, so my request 6 ("from the model, regardless of what's drawn") couldn't work as written. The approach is accepted and the spec is revised.
- **The flaw, by construction:** after the user swipes through track 1 to the end of the video, track 2 also starts at the end, so its early items come only by swiping back. QA checks this from Go to start: after the 20 clips, does "Caption 1" come next?
- **If not, the fix:** one scrollable node around all rows instead of one per row. TalkBack then reads each screen top to bottom, scrolls a screen and goes on, so every item comes in forward order.
- **Spec:** Accessibility → "Every item is reachable" and request 6.

**P6. The duration badge sticks to the visible left edge** like labels.
- **Where:** `TimelinePainter.kt:345-351` places the badge at `left + space-1`, so it scrolls off with the clip's start. While the user scrolls through a long selected clip, its length disappears.
- **Change:** place it at `max(left, 0f) + space-1`, and check its fit against the visible part. Spec Selection is updated.

**P7. Machine-draft wording** (P5-06 has the final say).
- **pt `a11y_state_original_muted`** (`values-pt/strings.xml:84`): "Áudio original sem som" reads as "original audio without sound". Change it to "Áudio original silenciado" (25/30).
- **pt `a11y_state_past_end`** (`:82`): "Parte depois do fim do vídeo" becomes "Em parte depois do fim do vídeo" (31/44), matching es.
- **es `track_meme_sound`** (`values-es/strings.xml:77`): "Sonido meme" becomes "Sonido de meme" (14/16), matching the Sounds tab's "Sonidos de memes".
- **pt `a11y_playhead`** "Cursor" can stay for now. P5-06 should check it against CapCut's pt-BR wording.
- **Low priority:** the id, es, pt and hi P1-05 comment blocks should point to the spec, as the English file does.

## Answers to the engineer's questions
1. **Ruler ladder: confirmed.** Use 15 min (3 minor ticks, every 5 min), 30 min (3, every 10 min) and 1 h (4, every 15 min), then whole hours.
   - **Why:** at "Show whole video", an hour-long project fills half of a 360 dp screen (0.05 dp per second). There a 10 min step is 30 dp, narrower than one "0:00:00" label.
   - **Result:** the build shows 00:00 · 30:00 · 1:00:00 with no overlap (render hour-long-whole).
   - **Past 1 h:** whole hours are fine. They only matter for projects of about 2.7 hours or more on a 360 dp phone.
   - **Spec updated:** Ruler → ladder, minor ticks, the reason, and a new table row.
2. **Zoom menu pane title: give it a new string** (P2). TalkBack speaks the pane title when the popup appears. "Timeline" sounds like nothing happened; "Zoom" says what opened. Key `timeline_zoom_menu`, English "Zoom", budget 16.
3. **Unnamed items: yes, add `a11y_item_unnamed`.**
   - English: `%1$s, %2$s long, starts at %3$s`. Budget 90.
   - Placeholders: %1$s is the track name; %2$s and %3$s are spoken times (P1-04 `a11y_time_*`).
   - Example: "Sticker, 2 seconds long, starts at 3.2 seconds".
   - **Why keep it after P1-08:** sounds get titles then, but a sticker the user cuts out (P2-07, used in video by P4-12) may have no name.
   - **Translations:** the engineer drafts id, es, pt and hi as `a11y_item` without the name slot.
4. **The playhead head over the first ruler label: acceptable, no fix.**
   - **Size:** at 0:00 the 12 dp head covers about 2 dp of the first "0" (renders open and hindi-200).
   - **Why accept it:**
     - Labels pass under the playhead all the time, because it's fixed and time moves.
     - The transport timecode just above shows the exact time.
     - The fixes cost more than they give. Moving every label about 10 dp off its tick loosens the label-tick link. A knockout ring around the head would be an outline. A taller ruler costs lane space.
   - **Spec:** Playhead → Layering now says so.
5. **Anything else:** M1 and P1–P7 above, and the notes below. Nothing breaks the v2 look: no hardcoded values, one blue only on sounds, white only for selection, no outlines, gradients or hard shadows.

## Notes (no change needed)
- **Selection clears at once** (no fade-out): approved. The spec Motion row now says "fade in; clearing removes them at once".
- **Lane add/remove motion** isn't in P1-05, because nothing here adds or removes a lane in use. It comes with P1-06 (delete), P1-08 (sound landing) and P1-09 (detach). The spec Motion row says so.
- **Handles overlap the neighbor clip.** On a selected main clip, the left handle covers about 11 dp of the previous clip's end (render main-clip-selected). That's expected from "handles outside the outline", and CapCut's handles overlap the same way (Assumption).
- **"More lanes below" cue.** The renders are 200 dp tall, and the last lane happened to end on the edge, so no lane was visibly cut. On the phone, `many-tracks` must show the bottom lane cut by the edge. If a lane boundary lines up with the edge there, tell me and I'll spec a cue.
- **Menu README** says "focus returns to the anchor on close". In P1 the menu opens only by touch and the ruler isn't focusable, so there's nothing to do until keyboard shortcuts (L-01).
- **Token docs:** `scrim` usage in `design/tokens.json` and DESIGN_SYSTEM now names its timeline uses (past-the-end wash, duration badge). There's no value change and no Kotlin change.
