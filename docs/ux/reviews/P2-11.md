# Design review: P2-11 (text shadow and background box)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 8 Style-panel renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P2-11/` (desktop, stand-in caption text, photo editor's host of the shared `StylePanel`), the reference frames in `docs/qa/references/P2-11-text-shadow-box/` (a desktop stand-in for the renderer, not Android's `StaticLayout` and `BlurMaskFilter`), the spec and the handoff. Nothing ran on a device, so the Done when (same caption in preview and export in both editors) is not judged here; this review covers the panel, the look of the rules, and the plan for the two unverified points.

## Verdict: Approved with polish (one must-fix before commit)

## What works
- **Style panel rows:** Shadow and Box follow Outline's grammar: "No shadow" and "No box" first (slashed circle, white ring when selected, hairline ring when not), then the colors. The Softness and Corners SegmentedChoice rows appear only while their effect is on, with Soft and Rounded preselected, white selected segment on the `canvas` track. The first screen of the panel is unchanged, as specified.
- **Bubble variant** (`70-style-panel-bubble-no-box-shadow-kept`): Shadow and Softness shown, no Box, Fill present.
- **The rules, in the references:**
  - One rectangle around the whole block (the two-line yellow box, the three-line white box), padding about 0.30 em by 0.18 em, Rounded corners at 0.25 em.
  - Shadow offset down and right at 60%, soft; the outlined caption (blue with a pink shadow) casts one silhouette.
  - The rotated caption's box and shadow turn with it (as decided).
  - The box clips at the canvas edge ("EDG").
  - The 2160 frames equal the 1080 frames scaled by 2.
  - The 40% group shows no overlap darkening between box, shadow and text.
  - On the black canvas black boxes vanish and white boxes read, as expected.
- **Selection frame** (`70-style-panel-box-on-corners`): it hugs the box, not the shadow, with a small even gap (the P1-10 frame padding).

## Must fix
- **M1. Corner segments break words at 200% in Spanish** (`70-spanish-200-style-panel-shadow-box`): "Cuadrada / s" and "Redonde / adas" split mid-word in half-width segments, which reads as a bug. SegmentedChoice says labels are never truncated and wrap into two rows, but didn't say what happens when one word is wider than a two-row segment. **Fix (rule added to `design/system/components/SegmentedChoice/README.md`):** if a label's longest word doesn't fit a two-row segment, the segments stack one per row at full width. This covers Softness and Corners in es and pt at 200% (and any later row). The Hindi frame (three segments in one row at 200%) is fine as is.

## Fix check (7 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** `70-spanish-200-style-panel-shadow-box` now stacks "Cuadradas", "Redondeadas" (selected, white) and "Píldora" one per row at full width, no word broken. (The top row is scrolled half out of view in the render, a scroll position, as in P3.)

## Final verdict: Approved with polish
The must-fix is closed. The unverified blur and the per-frame cost go to hand checks 6 and 11 with the pre-agreed fallback below.

## Polish
- **P1. The video editor's Style panel has no render.** The harness can't host it (README); the composable is shared, so the rows will match, but the video panel's height cap and scroll (the timeline region) are different from the photo's 40% cap. Device check: Shadow and Box are reachable by scrolling at 360 × 640 in the video editor, and the Softness row appearing doesn't jump the scroll position.
- **P2. Discoverability of Shadow and Box** at 360 × 640: both sit below Outline and need a scroll (spec's own assumption). Keep the QA question: do testers find them. If not, the fallback in the spec (a sub-panel) is a new ticket.
- **P3. Hindi 200% panel is scrolled so the top row is half cut** (`70-hindi-200-style-panel-shadow-box`). A scroll position, not a defect; confirm on the device that a row never rests half-visible at the end of a scroll (the last row must be fully reachable).
- **P4. Shadow on white text over white** ("THE END" on the white canvas) is nearly invisible by design of the test (white fill, light shadow). Keep it as a reference for "shadow alone doesn't make white text legible"; no change.

## The two unverified points
1. **Does `BlurMaskFilter` blur on the hardware canvas?** Android's hardware-acceleration table has long listed `Paint.setMaskFilter` as unsupported, so a blurred shadow drawn straight onto a hardware-accelerated canvas may come out sharp, and sharp on the photo screen but soft in the export (which breaks the Done when). Decision rule for hand check 6: if the shadow doesn't blur on the photo screen, draw the caption (box, shadow, outline, fill) once into a **software** bitmap and draw that bitmap, exactly as the video overlay already does. Don't ship the photo screen with a different shadow from the export.
2. **Per-frame blur on the photo screen.** Hand check 11 decides. The same cache solves it: key a caption bitmap by text, style, canvas size and layer scale (not by position or rotation), redraw it only when that key changes, and let a drag or turn only transform the bitmap. If check 11 shows janky frames while dragging a blurred caption, this is a must-fix, not polish; pre-agreed so it needs no new review. Expect the Done when's number (a drag under 5% janky frames) to be measured on the owner's phone.
