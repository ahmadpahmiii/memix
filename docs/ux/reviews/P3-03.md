# Design review: P3-03 Sound preview with waveform and details

Reviewed 11 Nov 2026 against `docs/ux/specs/P3-03-sound-preview-and-details.md` and my notes in `P3-02.md` and `P3-06.md`. Evidence: the 14 desktop renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P3-03/` (stand-in fonts, so Hindi and Spanish widths are approximate), the handoff, and the code (`SoundDetailsSheet`, `SoundWaveform`, `SoundRow`, `SoundsScreen` row focus, the volume toast path). No device, TalkBack, haptic or real-font run exists; those items are "code only" and go to the phase-end design pass.

## Verdict: Pass with polish

No must-fixes. Six polish items; items 1 to 3 are one-line changes I would take in this commit, the rest can wait for P3-04, P3-10 or P3-15.

## Builder decisions

| # | Decision | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Long press on the name block only | **Agree.** The whole-row long press would sit on top of the disc and the heart and would have no visible cue either way. The name block is also the tap target and the TalkBack "Show details" stop, so one control has one meaning. Spec amended (Entry and exit). Cost: a long press on the empty space right of a short name does nothing; acceptable, since a tap is the primary route. |
| 2 | Square-root display curve | **Agree, provisional.** The renders (`row-playing`, `details-ccby`, `details-cc0`) show the shapes read well: the decay on `details-cc0` is clear, quiet tails are still visible, and the played/unplayed split is easy to see. Re-check with the real starter waveforms in P3-12b: if most sounds look like a flat block, lower the exponent's effect or normalize by percentile. Nothing to do now. |
| 3 | Volume toast once per app run, 2 s, sound plays anyway | **Agree.** It fires only when media volume is zero (`LowVolumeWarningUseCase.isSilent`), so it does not nag users who are already hearing the sound. 2 s is short but the text is 5 words, and the Toast carries a polite live region (`Toast.kt:66`), so screen readers hear it. It is gated to the sheet while the sheet is open (`SoundDetailsSheet.kt:97`), so it does not show twice. One risk (Assumption): a user who mutes after the first run gets no second hint. That is the right trade against nagging; revisit only if the first test shows "app is broken" reports. |

## Known gaps: my ruling

- **Source link gap (visible in `details-ccby.png`, about 14 dp between "Source" and "freesound.org").** Polish 1. It breaks the spec's label-over-value rhythm (`space-3` between pairs, label hugging value).
- **Title not clamped to two lines (spec Layout item 1).** Polish 2. Low risk now (names are short, the Sheet title row grows), but a long My-sound name will push the player down. One line.

## Checks

**Tokens only: pass.** `SoundWaveform`, `SoundRail` and `SoundDetailsSheet` read colors, sizes, shapes and type through `MemixColors`, `MemixSize`, `MemixSpacing`, `MemixTheme`. The waveform tokens (`waveform-bar-width` 2, `waveform-bar-gap` 2, `waveform-height` 24, `waveform-height-large` 56) are in `design/tokens.json`, `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `MemixTokens.kt`. No v1 values, no outlines, shadows or gradients. One raw value remains: `SoundRow.kt:221` `size(36.dp)` for the add block. It is open from P3-06 (`sound-add-size` exists in tokens and DESIGN_SYSTEM); the Kotlin lands with P3-15 when the block appears. Tolerated.

**Played/unplayed contrast: pass.** `primary` #2BB3F3 on the playing-row band `surface-raised` #2C2C2C is about 5.9:1; `text-muted` #949494 on the same is about 4.6:1; both are higher on `surface` and `canvas`. Both clear 3:1 (SC 1.4.11). The two colors are close to each other (about 1.3:1), but the spec's rule holds: the split is also the position of the edge, and the renders show it plainly. Not a failure.

**Waveform (spec "The waveform").** Bars 2 dp with 2 dp gaps, rounded ends, centered on the midline, never shorter than their width. Row: only the playing or paused row shows it, under the name, at the text block's width (`row-playing.png`); idle rows show none. Paused keeps its position with the disc back to Play and the band kept (`row-paused.png`). No peaks falls back to the 4 dp rail (`row-playing-norails`, `details-norails`). Decorative for screen readers (`clearAndSetSemantics`). Progress is read in the draw phase only; the time text recomposes once a second. All as specced.

**States that can exist now.** Rendered and correct: row playing, paused, no peaks, hi at 200%; details with peaks (idle, playing, paused), CC BY with credit, CC0 without credit (Credit pair absent, nothing says "none"), no peaks, not saved online, source-failed toast, es, hi at 200%; the volume toast. Not rendered, and correctly waiting: downloading, failed, offline and removed (P3-04); My sound (not rendered; needs the P3-04 or P3-06 data, so I want `details-mine` at the P3-04 review); Use (P3-15); Report and reported (P3-10). The `sound_detail_saved` string exists but "Saved on this phone" is not rendered yet, only "Included with Memix"; QA should see both at P3-04.

**200% font.**
- Details sheet in Hindi (`details-hi-200.png`): nothing is clipped. The title, labels and values wrap, the credit takes three lines, Devanagari line height holds, the facts list is reachable (it scrolls). The Use and Report rows cannot be judged: they do not exist yet. The pinned-bar rule must be checked at P3-15 and P3-10.
- **The waveform is squeezed** to about a quarter of the row (disc, waveform, then the time at 200%). It still draws but is hard to read as a waveform. The spec says "the time may wrap under the waveform's row ... before anything is cut". Polish 3.
- Row in Hindi at 200% (`row-playing-hi-200.png`): meta wraps ("Royalty-" / "free"), the waveform stays under the name at text width, the band and heart hold. Pass.

**TalkBack (code only).**
- The name block is one merged stop "{name}, {meta}" with role Button and `onClickLabel` "Show details" (`SoundRow.kt:140`), and the long-press has the same label. The favorite custom action is kept on the merged node as P3-06 required (`SoundRow.kt:131-132`). Good.
- Heart in the sheet and the row: `Role.Switch`, "Favorite {name}", so it reads "on" or "off". Disc: "Play/Pause {name}". The waveform and the rail are hidden. Facts are merged label+value stops. The Source link's spoken name contains the host (SC 2.5.3).
- Focus return: `state.focusReturn` is matched per row and calls `detailsFocus.requestFocus()` (`SoundsScreen.kt:266-273`), guarded for rows that scrolled away. Right design. Needs a device check, because a Compose focus request does not always move TalkBack's accessibility focus; if it does not, the fallback is the top of the screen, which is not a trap but breaks the spec's "returns to the opener".
- Sheet title as a pane title or heading and TalkBack's move into the sheet on open: not visible in code I read (Sheet component); device check.

**Motion and reduce-motion.** Sheet slides over 200 ms (120 ms fade with reduce motion) via the shared Sheet. Disc and heart swap at once, as accepted in P3-06. The row waveform and time update from the player position; the spec's 4 updates a second with reduced motion is in `PlaybackClock`/`rememberPlaybackPosition`, which I did not trace; QA to confirm on a device with "Remove animations" on (the played edge should step, not glide).

## Polish

1. **Source link gap.** Make the label and the host one clickable block: put `Modifier.heightIn(min = touchTarget).clickable(...)` on the Column that holds both, with the label inside it, so the 48 dp target contains the label and the host and no dead space opens under the label. Keep the merged spoken name "Open {host} in your browser". (`SoundDetailsSheet.kt:160-175`.) Fix now.
2. **Clamp the sheet title to two lines** with an ellipsis (`maxLines = 2`) in the Sheet title slot (spec Layout item 1); the full name stays spoken via the semantics label. Fix now.
3. **Time under the waveform at large font.** When the font scale is above about 1.5 (or the waveform would be under 96 dp wide), put the time on its own line under the disc and waveform row, as the spec says. Spec: Accessibility, 200% font. Fix now if cheap; otherwise in P3-15 with the Use button, which also needs the 200% layout checked.
4. **Waveform bars are left-aligned** with the leftover width on the right (about 4 dp in `details-ccby.png` between the last bar and the time, with a gap that is not the same as at the disc side). Center the bar run in the width, or round the bar count so the run fills it. Cosmetic.
5. **Copy fixes** in the table above (id, pt, hi).
6. **Hand the missing states forward.** `details-mine`, `details-offline-notsaved`, `details-removed`, "Saved on this phone" and the downloading and failed player-row states are due in the P3-04 review; `details-hi-200` with Use and Report pinned is due in P3-15 and P3-10.
