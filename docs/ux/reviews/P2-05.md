# Design review: P2-05 part a (Draw)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 15 renders `95-*` (desktop, stand-in text), the stroke references in `docs/qa/references/P2-05-draw-censor/`, the spec and the handoff. Censor is 05b. Nothing ran on a device, so the stroke feel (no visible lag, smoothing, the eraser's clears) is for the device pass.

## Verdict: Changes requested (one must-fix)

## What works
- **The Draw bar** follows the spec: Brush and Eraser in a SegmentedChoice with Done at the end, the color swatches (white ring on the selected red), five size tiles in the white-fill selection look. The ratio pill is drawn disabled while drawing.
- **Strokes** (`draw-strokes-*`, the renders): smooth, round caps and joins, five clearly different sizes, a dot, a faded layer with no overlap darkening, and an eraser gap that clears only the drawing and not the picture beneath.
- **The drawing layer:** the selection frame is the strokes' bounding box with the corner handle; the Layers row says "Drawing" with a mini render of its strokes on a gray tile; the selected-drawing bar is Position, Opacity, Delete, Draw (pencil), Duplicate, with Delete in view at 360 dp. The main bar is Formats, Text, Add photo, Draw, Edit photo, Crop: the final order.
- **200% font** (es, hi): Brush and Eraser stack in two rows, size labels ("Finísimo", "अति पतला") fit their tiles, the tile row scrolls sideways, the bar scrolls inside its 40% cap.
- **The fifth size tile** (the builder's concern) does fit at 360 dp in the renders (its right edge at the 16 dp padding); it is only the Spanish and Hindi 200% rows that scroll, as specified.

## Must fix
- **M1. Four of the ten colors are hidden with no cue.** At 360 dp the swatch row shows exactly six whole swatches edge to edge (white, black, yellow, orange, red, pink), so nothing suggests that purple, blue, light blue and green exist (`95-draw-bar-brush`, `-360x640`). The spec says the row scrolls "edge to edge" so a partly cut swatch is the cue, and this build lands on an exact fit. Fix (spec amended): place the swatches at a pitch that leaves the next one partly cut (about 54 dp apart at 360 dp), and scroll the selected color into view when Draw mode opens. Same rule in the other swatch rows when they overflow.

## Polish
- **P1. The bar changes height between Brush and Eraser** (`95-draw-bar-brush` against `95-draw-bar-eraser`): hiding the color row shrinks the bar by about 56 dp and the canvas moves about 28 dp while the user is mid-drawing. The preview should not jump. Keep the bar's height the same: in Eraser mode put one line of `body` `text-secondary` in the color row's place ("Rub out parts of your drawing", `draw_eraser_hint`, budget 40) so the canvas stays put.
- **P2. Thin and Fine look the same** (the 3 dp minimum dot). Draw the five dots on a perceptual scale, 4, 7, 10, 15 and 22 dp (spec amended) so the order is readable at a glance; the canvas shows the true size.
- **P3. The Layers-row thumbnail of an all-erased drawing** (untested): a drawing whose brush strokes were fully erased renders to a blank tile. If the mini render has no visible pixel, draw the `draw` icon centered on the tile in `surface`/`text-muted` so the row isn't empty.
- **P4. A palm or second finger is ignored** by design; confirm on the device that a resting palm never makes a mark and that a stroke started at the stage edge stays inside the canvas clip.
- **P5. Freehand has no alternative for a screen-reader user** (an essential drag, WCAG 2.5.7's exception; the spec states it). The undo, layer opacity, Position and Delete panels remain single-pointer.

## Fix check (7 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** `95-draw-bar-brush-360x640` shows the swatches at the 52 dp pitch (48 plus `space-1`, a token gap, so my 54 dp was the wrong figure): red is scrolled into view, and swatches are partly cut on both sides (the previous one on the left, light blue on the right), so the row plainly scrolls.
- **P1 fixed.** Brush and Eraser (`95-draw-bar-*-360x640`) have the same bar height and the canvas sits at the same place in both; the Eraser's hint reads "Rubs out your drawing, not the picture."
- **P2 fixed.** The five dots are now clearly five sizes; Thin and Fine are apart.
- **P3: accepted on the code review.** No render of an all-erased row; the `draw` icon fallback is checked on the device.

## Final verdict, part a: Approved with polish
P4 and P5 and the device-pass list stand.

## For the device pass (QA)
- The stroke follows the finger with no visible lag at 60 fps; a 3-minute scribble stays within the saved-size note the builder reports; the 5,000-point cap toast ("This drawing is full…").
- One stroke is one undo step ("Undo: Draw", "Undo: Erase"); undoing the stroke that made the layer removes it and Draw mode stays open.
- Draw on a rotated, scaled or opacity-reduced drawing layer; Duplicate offsets 3% of S; a ratio change keeps strokes proportioned.
- The eraser first (nothing drawn) toasts and records nothing.
- Preview, render hook and export equal (the references are the stroke renders at 1080 and 2160); Hindi at 200% with the bar scrolled.
