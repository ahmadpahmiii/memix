# Design review: P2-05 part a (Draw)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 15 renders `95-*` (desktop, stand-in text), the stroke references in `docs/qa/references/P2-05-draw-censor/`, the spec and the handoff. Censor is 05b. Nothing ran on a device, so the stroke feel (no visible lag, smoothing, the eraser's clears) is for the device pass.

## Verdict: Changes requested (one must-fix)

## What works
- **The Draw bar** follows the spec: Brush and Eraser in a SegmentedChoice with Done at the end, the color swatches (white ring on the selected red), five size tiles in the white-fill selection look. The ratio pill is drawn disabled while drawing.
- **Strokes** (`draw-strokes-*`, the renders): smooth, round caps and joins, five clearly different sizes, a dot, a faded layer with no overlap darkening, and an eraser gap that clears only the drawing and not the picture beneath.
- **The drawing layer:** the selection frame is the strokes' bounding box with the corner handle; the Layers row says "Drawing" with a mini render of its strokes on a gray tile; the selected-drawing bar is Position, Opacity, Delete, Draw (pencil), Duplicate, with Delete in view at 360 dp. The main bar is Formats, Text, Add photo, Draw, Edit photo, Crop: the final order.
- **200% font** (es, hi): Brush and Eraser stack in two rows, size labels ("Finísimo", "अति पतला") fit their tiles, the tile row scrolls sideways, the bar scrolls inside its 40% cap.
- **The fifth size tile** (the builder's concern) does fit at 360 dp in the renders (its right edge at the 16 dp padding); it is only the Spanish and Hindi 200% rows that scroll, as specified.

## Must fix
- **M1. Four of the ten colors are hidden with no cue.** At 360 dp the swatch row shows exactly six whole swatches edge to edge (white, black, yellow, orange, red, pink), so nothing suggests that purple, blue, light blue and green exist (`95-draw-bar-brush`, `-360x640`). The spec says the row scrolls "edge to edge" so a partly cut swatch is the cue, and this build lands on an exact fit. Fix (spec amended): place the swatches at a pitch that leaves the next one partly cut (about 54 dp apart at 360 dp), and scroll the selected color into view when Draw mode opens. Same rule in the other swatch rows when they overflow.

## Polish
- **P1. The bar changes height between Brush and Eraser** (`95-draw-bar-brush` against `95-draw-bar-eraser`): hiding the color row shrinks the bar by about 56 dp and the canvas moves about 28 dp while the user is mid-drawing. The preview should not jump. Keep the bar's height the same: in Eraser mode put one line of `body` `text-secondary` in the color row's place ("Rub out parts of your drawing", `draw_eraser_hint`, budget 40) so the canvas stays put.
- **P2. Thin and Fine look the same** (the 3 dp minimum dot). Draw the five dots on a perceptual scale, 4, 7, 10, 15 and 22 dp (spec amended) so the order is readable at a glance; the canvas shows the true size.
- **P3. The Layers-row thumbnail of an all-erased drawing** (untested): a drawing whose brush strokes were fully erased renders to a blank tile. If the mini render has no visible pixel, draw the `draw` icon centered on the tile in `surface`/`text-muted` so the row isn't empty.
- **P4. A palm or second finger is ignored** by design; confirm on the device that a resting palm never makes a mark and that a stroke started at the stage edge stays inside the canvas clip.
- **P5. Freehand has no alternative for a screen-reader user** (an essential drag, WCAG 2.5.7's exception; the spec states it). The undo, layer opacity, Position and Delete panels remain single-pointer.

## Fix check (7 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** `95-draw-bar-brush-360x640` shows the swatches at the 52 dp pitch (48 plus `space-1`, a token gap, so my 54 dp was the wrong figure): red is scrolled into view, and swatches are partly cut on both sides (the previous one on the left, light blue on the right), so the row plainly scrolls.
- **P1 fixed.** Brush and Eraser (`95-draw-bar-*-360x640`) have the same bar height and the canvas sits at the same place in both; the Eraser's hint reads "Rubs out your drawing, not the picture."
- **P2 fixed.** The five dots are now clearly five sizes; Thin and Fine are apart.
- **P3: accepted on the code review.** No render of an all-erased row; the `draw` icon fallback is checked on the device.

## Final verdict, part a: Approved with polish
P4 and P5 and the device-pass list stand.

## Device pass, part a (QA)
- The stroke follows the finger with no visible lag at 60 fps; a 3-minute scribble stays within the saved-size note the builder reports; the 5,000-point cap toast ("This drawing is full…").
- One stroke is one undo step ("Undo: Draw", "Undo: Erase"); undoing the stroke that made the layer removes it and Draw mode stays open.
- Draw on a rotated, scaled or opacity-reduced drawing layer; Duplicate offsets 3% of S; a ratio change keeps strokes proportioned.
- The eraser first (nothing drawn) toasts and records nothing.
- Preview, render hook and export equal (the references are the stroke renders at 1080 and 2160); Hindi at 200% with the bar scrolled.

# Part b (Censor)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 8 frames `96-*`, the censor references (`censor-modes-photo`, `censor-strengths-photo`, `censor-order`, the legibility card), the spec and the handoff. Nothing ran on a device.

## Verdict: Changes requested (two must-fixes)

## What works
- **The panel:** Mode tiles (Blur, Pixelate, Black bar with their icons), Shape tiles (Rectangle, Ellipse), Strength below; the selected tile in the white fill; Hindi at 200% holds. The Black bar has no Strength row (`96-censor-panel-bar`).
- **The layer:** pixelated, blurred and black areas draw under the layers above them, so "SHARP ABOVE" stays sharp over a censor (`censor-modes-photo`); the pixel grid is aligned to the canvas; a rounded ellipse blur and a hard-edged rectangle; no Opacity anywhere for a censor.
- **Handles:** corner handle plus four edge bars (20 by 6 dp), the two-tone frame; the Position panel has Width and Height steppers (the single-pointer route).
- **Layers row:** the censor icon on a tile like a text row.

## 1. Tokens: accepted
`edge-handle-length` (20 dp) and `edge-handle-thickness` (6 dp) are the right tokens, the values are the spec's, and the names fit the scheme (`MemixSize.edgeHandleLength` and `edgeHandleThickness`). They stay as built. Reuse them if the Crop screen's edge bars (P2-04) are redrawn from tokens later; no change now.

## Must fix
- **M1. Legibility: the minimums are too weak for a box that fits its text.** The card passes because its text is small and its strip is thin, which is the case the floors rescue. The real risk is a snug box: text 0.06 S tall in a box about 0.08 S tall, at strength 0.
  - **Blur:** sigma was 0.12 × a with a 0.012 S floor, so about 0.012 S against 0.06 S text, a ratio of a fifth. Blur that light leaves large text readable. The thin-strip case is fine because the floor is large against 0.03 S text (a ratio of 0.4).
  - **Pixelate:** a block of a/8 across a snug box is about 8 blocks across the text line, and an 8-block-tall line of digits is readable (this is what mosaic readers recover).
  - **Fix (spec and both token files updated):** Pixelate blocks **a × (1/5 at 0 to 1/2 at 100%)**, floor 0.012 S (at most 5 blocks across the area); Blur sigma **a × (0.22 to 0.5)**, floor **0.016 S**. The legibility card gets more cases: 0.03 S, 0.06 S and 0.10 S text, each in a snug box (1.3 × the text height) and a loose one (3×), Blur and Pixelate at 0; two people must not be able to read any. Re-render the card and the sheets after the change (the look moves; nothing is saved yet, so no migration). The weakest case the builder named, Blur over a thin strip, is acceptable as drawn (the smear shows blobs, no digit), and now sits at 0.016 S.
- **M2. Handles overlap on a small box, and the nearest-wins rule isn't built.** The spec line 83 says the nearest handle wins; the handoff says it isn't. On a box at the 0.06 S minimum (about 24 dp on a 360 dp canvas) the four 48 dp edge targets and the corner handle cover the whole box, so a one-finger drag can't move it and a touch near the corner may grab a side. Fix (spec amended): hit-test by the **nearest handle centre**, and **cap a handle's target at a third of the box's side on screen (minimum 24 dp)** so the middle of the box stays a move area. The Width and Height steppers remain the route when the box is too small to grab.

## Polish
- **P1. Strength is below the fold at 360 × 640** (`96-censor-panel-360x640`: only its label and "0%" show). Order the panel **Mode, Strength, Shape**: strength belongs to the mode and the user changes it far more often than the shape, and the slider then stays in sight.
- **P2. Strength readout and defaults:** the renders show 0% from the legibility setup; the default for a new censor stays 50% (check on the device).
- **P3. Blur is an approximation** (downscaled copy enlarged bilinearly, not an exact Gaussian); fine for a censor, as long as the rule above (sigma as the minimum) holds in the real pixels. QA's two-people test decides, on a phone render, not only the desktop one.

## Fix check, part b (7 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** I opened the new cards at 1080 and 2160 (`censor-legibility-text003`, `-text006`, `-text010`, and the card): in every case, snug and loose, Blur and Pixelate at strength 0, no digit can be told apart. Blur is a smooth smear with no stroke structure left; Pixelate leaves two to four rows of large mid-gray blocks with no letter shape (the 0.06 S snug strip is the tightest, three rows, and reads as noise). The 2160 frames equal the 1080 ones scaled. Passed on the desktop render; the two-people test on a phone render stays the final word.
- **M2: accepted as built, with a condition.** A target capped at a third of the side (24 dp minimum) keeps the four edge targets apart from each other and leaves the middle third of the box as a move area, so a separate nearest-centre test isn't needed down to a box of about 72 dp. Below that, the targets can crowd the box and the corner handle can sit over a side: the Width and Height steppers, the Move row and two-finger gestures are the route, as the spec already says. Condition for QA: on a 0.06 S to 0.12 S box, grab each side and the corner and move the box by two fingers; if a touch near the corner grabs the wrong thing at 72 dp or more, add the nearest-centre test.
- **P1 fixed.** Mode, Strength, Shape: at 360 × 640 the Strength slider is in view (`96-censor-panel-360x640`).

## Final verdict, part b: Approved with polish
P2 and P3 stand. The two-people legibility test on a phone render is the last gate before the censor is called safe.

## For the device pass (QA)
- The two-people legibility test from the spec on the updated card, in the 1080 export of a phone render, Blur and Pixelate at 0.
- The pixel grid and the blur are the same in the editor, the render hook and the export, at 1080 and 2160 (grid in units of S).
- Drag each edge handle; a tiny box (0.06 S): move by dragging the middle, resize with the Position panel; a rotated censor resizes along its own axes.
- Lock a censor: handles replaced by the badge, tools explain. A hidden layer under a censor isn't drawn (not censored); a locked one is.
- Undo names: "Add censor"; ids on add and on mode change.
