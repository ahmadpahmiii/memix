# Design review · P1-02 Gallery picker and media import

**Verdict: Approved with polish. Provisional, from code** (8 Oct 2026). The final check uses the owner's screenshots at end-of-phase QA (owner's decision on review mode). The screenshot list is in `docs/qa/phase-1/engineer-hand-checks.md` → P1-02.

Reviewed commit 0d6ea30 (CI green) against `docs/ux/specs/P1-02-gallery-picker.md`. There are no screenshots: this cloud session has no emulator. I read these files:

## Matches the spec
- **States.** Every Memix state exists and can be reached:
  - copying, with time left (only after 2 s and only over 10 s; 5 s steps, then whole minutes)
  - copying a file of unknown size ("48 MB copied")
  - over the limit (the sheet shows at once)
  - cancelled; some didn't go in (limit only, failures only, both); none went in
  - not enough space (before copying, and while copying), with the "no button" variant
  - no picker; quick path straight to the editor
- **Waits.** The sheet appears only after 300 ms and stays at least 500 ms. Percent rounds down and stops at 99 until the copy is done. Progress updates at most 10 times a second.
- **Picker.** It ignores a second tap while launching, so a double tap can't open two pickers. It asks for images and videos with ordered selection, and no accent color is set.
- **Scrim.** One scrim is shared by the Create sheet and the import sheet, so it doesn't blink at the hand-over. From Home, the scrim fades in with the sheet.
- **Layout and components.** Blocking Sheet variant (no grabber, close button or drag; scrim taps do nothing). Title, then `space-4`, then the count/percent row, `space-2`, ProgressBar (`rail-height`, `hairline` rail, `primary` fill), `space-2`, the detail line, `space-6`, then the full-width secondary Cancel. Results have the body, the rows (type icon at 24 in `text-secondary`, name with the ellipsis in the middle, reason that wraps) and one full-width button. A long list scrolls between the body and the button, and the sheet stops `space-10` below the status bar.
- **One blue per screen.** During copying, only the progress fill is blue. Continue, Pick again and Free up space are primary; Cancel and Close are secondary. Nothing is selected, so there's no white. No outlines, gradients or hard shadows.
- **Tokens only.** The new UI files have no color, dp, sp, font or shape literals. `MemixSize.railHeight` exists. Motion uses the `MemixMotion` durations.
- **Copy.** English matches the spec word for word. All strings are within budget in all five languages, including the longest ones:
  - pt `import_none_title`: 32/32
  - pt `import_reason_unsupported`: 57/60
  - es `import_storage_body` with sizes filled in: about 110/130

  Placeholders are numbered, and Hindi puts the total first. Sizes follow the language (decimal comma in id, es and pt; non-breaking space before the unit). es writes the percent as "40 %". `create_video_body` is "Pick videos and photos" in all five languages.
- **Accessibility:**
  - The title is the pane title, so it's read on open and on every change to a result.
  - The count line is a polite live region; percent and time left aren't live.
  - The ProgressBar has range info plus a state description.
  - Each row that didn't go in is one node: "{name}, {Video or Photo}. {reason}".
  - Editor rows read `editor_media_row_a11y`.
  - Every button is at least `touch-target` tall, and button labels and titles wrap.
  - At large font sizes, the percent wraps under the count (FlowRow).
- **Motion.**
  - The sheet slides in over 200 ms; with reduce motion it fades over 120 ms.
  - Going from copying to a result, the content cross-fades over 120 ms and the height changes over 200 ms; with reduce motion the height snaps.
  - The progress fill moves over 120 ms, linear; with reduce motion it jumps.
  - Pick again closes the sheet, waits 200 ms, then opens the picker.
- **Back and Escape.** Before the sheet shows, back cancels the copy. On the copying sheet, back is the same as Cancel; on "Some didn't go in", back continues to the editor. On every other result, back closes the sheet.
- **Editor placeholder.** It reads clips back from the saved project. Each row has the position in `timecode` `text-muted`, the type icon, the type label in `body-strong`, and `00:12.40`-style durations, right-aligned. Rows are at least `touch-target` tall with `space-4` gutters.

## Accepted, not a defect
- **N1.** The import sheet slides away instead of being covered by the editor's 320 ms push (spec → Motion, "To the editor"). Owner decision: accept for now; fixed in P1-04.

## Polish
1. **Failed first save (owner decision, 8 Oct; spec States → "Ran out of space saving the new project").** Today any save failure shows "Couldn't add your media" with no rows and deletes the copies (`MediaImportViewModel.kt:121-126`). On `StorageFull`:
   - keep the batch and show `NotEnoughSpace` (it needs the 100 MB headroom)
   - on resume with enough space, save again without copying again, then go to the editor or "Some didn't go in"
   - Close deletes the copies

   The spec was written after the build, so this isn't a defect in 0d6ea30. It must land before end-of-phase QA (in the next engineer commit, alongside P1-04), because the current message tells the user to "try other videos or photos" when the phone is full.
2. **Focus after a result closes** (spec → Accessibility, "focus returns to the entry card or row when the sheet closes without a pick"). Nothing returns focus today (no `FocusRequester` in `App.kt` or `VideoMemeImport.kt`). After Close on "None went in", "Not enough space" or "No picker", put focus on the Home Video meme card, or on the nav Create button when the pick started in the Create sheet (that sheet has closed by then). QA checks it in the keyboard and TalkBack pass.
3. **Spoken row punctuation is built in code** (`ImportSheet.kt:255-257`: ", " and ". "). Move it into a string resource (`import_row_a11y`, "%1$s, %2$s. %3$s") so Hindi can use "।". Low priority; the P5-06 translation pass at the latest.

## Spec corrected to match the build
- **Free up space.** The spec said the button asks for "the missing amount"; the build sends the total needed, headroom included. Android documents `EXTRA_REQUESTED_BYTES` as the total the app will allocate, so the build is right. Spec line updated.

## For QA to confirm on screen (end of phase)
- Spanish percent shows "40 %" with a real space, not a literal ` `. Compose resources decode `\uXXXX`, and the file-size strings already rely on it.
- Hindi at 200% on `copying-hi-200` and `some-not-added-hi-200`: the title wraps, the percent wraps under the count, and nothing clips (Devanagari line height).
- Tapping Cancel during the last 500 ms after a copy ends does nothing: the project may already be saved. That's correct, but note it if it reads as broken.
