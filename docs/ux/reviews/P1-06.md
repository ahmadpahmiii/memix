## Matches the spec

  - Main tool bar: Edit only (P1-08 and P1-10 add theirs). Edit selects the main clip under the playhead, or the last one at the very end.
  - Clip tool bar: a 48 dp Close clip tools button (`arrow_back`), a `stroke-hairline` × `space-6` divider in `hairline`, then the tools, scrolling sideways. Main clips: Split · Delete · Duplicate · Reorder. Text: Delete · Split · Duplicate. Everything else: Split · Delete · Duplicate. Delete is third at most, so "Split, then Delete" needs no scroll at 360 dp (render 51).
  - Look: `surface`, at least `toolbar-height`, `hairline` top border. Tools in `text-secondary`; Delete icon and label in `danger`. Pressed fills fade within the same gray (no pass through black).
  - Main ↔ clip ↔ reorder bar: a 120 ms cross-fade.
  - Tools that can't act keep their look and explain on tap: Split outside the clip (render 55), Delete and Reorder on the only clip, Move earlier and later at the ends.
  - No dead controls: Volume, Detach audio, Move here, Meme sounds and Replace aren't drawn before their tickets.
- **Edits:**
  - Split selects the right piece. Delete clears the selection and the main tool bar returns. Duplicate selects the copy and bonks it (render 63 shows the bonk; the outline sits on the original there only because the render isn't wired to the ViewModel).
  - The main track ripples over 200 ms (render 62). Other lanes keep their times (render 46).
  - Each edit is one undo step, and `tool_use` fires once per recorded step, never on a no-op or an explanation (engineer's JVM harness, 112/112).
- **Trim:**
  - Handles drag at once and win over scrolling. Only the moving edge moves; the main track shows the gap or overlap until release (renders 40, 41), then closes up (render 46).
  - Limits: footage end with one SegmentTick (render 41), a neighbor on the same lane as a hard stop (render 44), 0.1 s minimum.
  - Snapping to the playhead (no guide, render 42), to clip and item edges with the `hairline`-wide `text-secondary` guide across the lanes (renders 43, 44). One tick per engagement.
  - Value bubble: `surface-raised`, `radius-full`, `timecode` in `text`, `space-1`/`space-2` padding, centered on the moving edge and kept inside the width; 120 ms fade-in.
  - The preview shows the frame at the moving edge, then returns to the playhead on release. Back cancels the drag.
  - Edge auto-scroll near both sides.
- **Reorder:** long-press lift with a LongPress haptic, or the Reorder tool. Tiles of `track-height-video` at a `space-1` gap, the moving one outlined in white, a `scrim` wash over the rest (renders 56, 60, 61). The moving tile starts under the finger or under the playhead. Drop or Done commits one step; an unchanged order records nothing; back cancels. The playhead goes to the moved clip's start.

- **Tokens only:** the audit finds nothing in `:feature:video-editor` or `ToolBar.kt`: no `Color(0x…)`, dp, sp, `FontFamily`, `RoundedCornerShape`, `Brush`, `border` or v1 hex. The 64 dp item width is built from tokens (question 3 gives it its own).

No outlines around shapes, no gradients, no hard shadows. The only soft shadows are the toast's and the bubble's (P1 removes the bubble's).
- **Accessibility:**

  - The selected item carries Split, Delete, Duplicate, and on main clips Move earlier and Move later, as actions. Handles read "Start of clip 3", value "9.0 seconds", adjustable by one frame, with "Trim to playhead" (semantics.txt).
  - After each edit a polite, invisible announcement ("Delete: clip 2"). Reorder tiles read "Video, clip 5 of 20" with the moving one selected, and each move announces "Clip 6 of 20".
  - Targets: tools at least 64 × 48, Close 48, reorder buttons at least 48 tall, handles a full `touch-target` wide.

## Must fix before end-of-phase QA
**M1. In reorder mode, the washed areas respond.**
- **Where:** `TimelineGestures.kt:110-113`. In reorder mode the detector passes only `down.position.x` to `onGrab` and `onTap`, and `TimelineController.grabMovingTile` (`:217`) and `tapReorderTile` (`:245`) look up tiles by x alone.
- **The problem:** a press anywhere in the moving tile's column (on the washed ruler or the audio lane, say) grabs it. A tap anywhere else at another tile's x moves the clip to that place. Tapping the washed area is exactly what people do to get out of a mode, so the clip jumps to wherever the finger happened to be. Done then commits an order the user never chose. That breaks "Forgiving" and the spec line below.
- **Fix:**
  - Give `detectReorderGestures` a parameter `isOnStrip: (Offset) -> Boolean`.
  - In its `isReordering()` branch, right after `down.consume()`, add `if (!isOnStrip(down.position)) { tapUntilUp(down, onTap = {}); return@awaitEachGesture }`. `tapUntilUp` consumes every change until the finger lifts, so nothing below scrolls or taps, and the empty `onTap` does nothing.
  - In `Timeline.kt:205-215`, pass `isOnStrip = { offset -> mainRow != null && offset.y >= rulerHeightPx + mainRow.topPx - laneScroll.value && offset.y < rulerHeightPx + mainRow.topPx + mainRow.heightPx - laneScroll.value }`, with `mainRow` found as in `ReorderSemantics` (`lanes.rows.firstOrNull { it.track.kind == TrackKind.MAIN_VIDEO }`). That's the 48 dp row: tiles are 40 dp drawn in 48 dp targets (spec → Touch targets).
  - No new string, no new token.
- **Spec line:** Reorder → In reorder mode: "The ruler, the other lanes, the leading and trailing areas and the add row get a `scrim` wash and don't respond." Now spelled out: only the strip row takes touches; elsewhere a touch is taken and does nothing.

**M2. In tap mode, the moving tile can slide off the screen.**
- **Where:** `ReorderStrip.showOrder` (`ReorderStrip.kt:85-93`) slides the tiles but never moves `originPx`. The strip scrolls only through edge auto-scroll while dragging (`TimelineController.kt:261-272`), and in reorder mode a swipe off the moving tile does nothing (`TimelineGestures.kt:150-160`).
- **The problem:** from the Reorder tool, the moving tile starts under the playhead with about four tiles of room on each side (44 dp pitch, 180 dp half-width). From about the fifth tap of Move later or Move earlier, it leaves the screen and keeps moving, unseen. Tiles past the edge can't be tapped either. That fails the first usability heuristic, visibility of system status ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/), Guidance). QA's 20-clip project shows it at once; so does any project with more than about eight clips. My spec didn't cover it.
- **Fix:**
  - In `showOrder`, when `!isDragging`, work out the moving tile's new left edge: `originPx + order.indexOf(movingId) * pitchPx`.
  - If it's less than `edgeZonePx` (`touch-target`) from the left, shift `originPx` right by the difference. If its right edge is less than `edgeZonePx` from the right side of the timeline, shift `originPx` left by the difference.
  - Animate `originPx` over `MemixMotion.durationSheet` together with the slide (snap with reduce motion), within the same clamp as `scrollBy`.
  - `showOrder` needs the timeline width and `edgeZonePx`: pass them from `FollowReorderMode` (`state.widthPx`, `controller.edgeZonePx`).
  - No new string, no new token.
- **Spec line:** Reorder → In reorder mode: "The selected clip's tile is under … the playhead (taps)", and Moving by taps. Now: "When the mode starts …", plus a new rule, "The moving tile always stays in view".

## Polish
**P1. The value bubble loses its shadow.**
- **Where:** `TrimDrag.kt:194`.
- **Change:** remove `.dropShadow(MemixShapes.radiusFull, MemixElevation.shadowFloat)`, and the two imports if nothing else uses them.
- **Why:** on `canvas` the shadow is invisible. Where it lands on content, it reads as dirt: a gray falloff on the white handle (renders 40, 41, 43) and a brown smudge on the orange caption (render 44). The `surface-raised` value step separates it, as the duration badge does without a shadow.
- **Spec:** Bubbles and guides updated.

**P2. The value bubble stays inside the timeline's top edge.**
- **Where:** `TrimDrag.kt:206`.
- **Change:** end the `y` expression with `.coerceAtLeast(0)`.
- **Why:** on the main row, `y` is −4 dp (ruler 24 + row top 0 − gap 4 − bubble 24). The render window cuts the bubble's top corners; on the phone it would overlap the transport row's hairline. When lanes are scrolled so the dragged row is half under the ruler, it would float over the stage. Clamped, the main-row bubble sits at the top of the timeline over the ruler, still clear of the outline.

**P3. The reorder bar gets the tool bar's hairline.**
- **Where:** `EditorToolBar.kt:88-92` (`ReorderBar`).
- **Change:** add `.drawBehind { drawRect(MemixColors.hairline, Offset.Zero, Size(size.width, MemixStroke.strokeHairline.toPx())) }` before the padding, the same line as `ToolBar.kt:98`.
- **Why:** without it the bar's top edge disappears for the length of reorder mode (render 56 against 51).

**P4. The playhead fades out in reorder mode.**
- **Where:** `Timeline.kt:297`, and `TimelinePainter.drawPlayhead` (`:499`).
- **Change:** give `drawPlayhead` an `alpha` parameter for both the line and the head, and pass `1f - (state.reorder?.collapse?.value ?: 0f)`. It fades out with the wash and back in as the tiles expand.
- **Why:** tiles aren't laid out in time. A full-white line through a tile (renders 56, 60) reads as a second selection mark next to the white-outlined moving tile.

**P5. Tool labels are never cut.**
- **Where:** `ToolBar.kt:142` (and the `MAX_LABEL_LINES` constant).
- **Change:** drop `maxLines = MAX_LABEL_LINES`.
- **Why:** an item is only as wide as the longest word of its label (`IntrinsicSize.Min`). So at 200% a three-word label breaks into one word per line, and the third line is cut. Nothing in P1-06 has three words, but P1-08's Meme sounds will ("Sonidos de meme", "Sons de meme"). Two lines is the copy budget, not a cap, the same rule as the Toast (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.4). The ToolBar README, DESIGN_SYSTEM and spec now say so.

**P6. Focus follows the bar when reorder mode starts from the Reorder tool.**
- **Where:** `EditorToolBar.kt` (`ReorderBar`, `rememberClipTools`).
- **Change:** when reorder mode starts from the tool (`byDrag` false), request focus on Move earlier through a `FocusRequester`. When it ends (Done, back), request it on the Reorder tool in the clip tool bar that comes back. Check on the phone that TalkBack's focus follows.
- **Why:** the focused Reorder button disappears in the cross-fade, and TalkBack is left with nothing. Focus after a surface swap is the item I see missed most often (P1-02, P1-04).

**P7. Copy** (machine drafts; P5-06 has the final say):
- **pt `explain_reorder_one`** is 55/50: "Para reordenar, você precisa de pelo menos dois clipes." Change it to "Reordenar exige pelo menos dois clipes." (39/50).
- **id `tool_reorder` and `edit_reorder`:** "Urutkan" reads as "Sort". Change both to "Atur urutan" (11/20 and 11/24; the tool label wraps to two lines). Then change id `explain_reorder_one` to match: "Mengatur urutan perlu setidaknya dua klip." (42/50).
- **hi `tool_delete` and `edit_delete`:** "डिलीट" while P1-04's toast `editor_missing_media` tells users to "हटाएं" that clip. The toast names the tool, so they must match. Change both to "हटाएं" (the native verb, already used in the toast).
- **For P5-06:** the other Hindi transliterations (स्प्लिट, डुप्लिकेट) against Android's own Hindi wording, and pt "Cortar" (trim) next to "Dividir" (split).

## Follow-ups (not blocking P1-06)
**P8. Extending the last clip stops at the screen edge** (question 4c).
- **The rule to build:** while a handle is held, the scroll may go past the end of the video, as far as that handle's own limit. On release the view settles at the new end. Spec → Edge auto-scroll → Past the end while trimming.
- **Why it can wait:** nothing is lost, and the way round is easy (release and drag again, or zoom out). It does cap each drag at half a screen (about 4.5 s at the default zoom), and at the edge the handle just stops with no tick, which feels stuck. Build it in P1-06's fix pass if the slot allows; otherwise the PM schedules it before the phase gate.

**P9. Real reduce-motion fades before iOS** (question 4b). On iOS (P7) the ripple, reorder collapse, expand and slides must become the spec's 120 ms cross-fades. On Android they can't be seen anyway (question 4b).

## Answers to the engineer's questions
1. **Reorder bar: confirmed as built, and added to the spec** (Reorder → Reorder bar).
   - **Layout:** Move earlier and Move later as Secondary buttons, in that order from the left, so each button's side matches its direction. Done as a Quiet button after them. The row wraps at large fonts and the bar grows; nothing is cut.
   - **Why Done is blue:** it's the mode's one confirming action. Done commits the order and back cancels it. A ToolPanel's Done (Ghost, white) only closes a panel whose changes already applied, so the two Dones may differ. Blue text without a fill keeps it below a Primary button, and nothing else is blue in reorder mode, because the meme sounds sit under the wash.
   - **Words, not icon tools:** no "earlier" or "later" icon exists, and words carry the direction in all five languages.
   - **Two additions:** the hairline top border (P3), and focus moving to Move earlier (P6).
2. **The three strings: approved as drafted.** English exact copy, now in the spec's copy table:
   - `a11y_clip_label`: `clip %1$d` (budget 12). Lowercase, because it always sits mid-sentence: "Start of clip 3", "Delete: clip 2".
   - `a11y_reorder_tile`: `%1$s, clip %2$d of %3$d` (budget 40). %1$s is "Video" or "Photo". Example: "Video, clip 2 of 3".
   - `a11y_reorder_position`: `Clip %1$d of %2$d` (budget 24). Example: "Clip 2 of 3".
   - The drafts in id, es, pt and hi are fine; Hindi reorders the placeholders correctly.
3. **Yes: a named token, `tool-item-width` = 64px.**
   - "Touch target plus two `space-2`" is arithmetic that happens to give 64, not the intent. The intent is "about five and a half tools at 360 dp", and the photo editor's bar (P2) uses it too.
   - My spec wrote "64 dp", which is a raw value the system didn't have; that was my slip.
   - **Added to:** `design/tokens.json`, `design/system/tokens.json` and DESIGN_SYSTEM → Size, logged in `docs/ux/CHANGELOG.md`.
   - **Engineer:** add `MemixSize.toolItemWidth = 64.dp`, and replace `ToolItemWidth` in `ToolBar.kt:153` with it.
   - **Mirror:** `design/system/tokens.json` had missed the P1-10 additions (the `on-primary` edge note, four caption font files, `caption-sample`, `canvas-handle-size`, `swatch-size`, the `caption` group). It now matches `design/tokens.json` again. There's no Kotlin change from that sync.
4. **Known gaps:**
   - **(a) The bubble has no fade-out: accept.** It goes at once on release because the committed edit takes over (ripple, preview), the same way the selection clears (P1-05 review). The snap guide's lack of a fade is accepted too: on and off at once, with the tick, marks the exact snap moment. Spec updated.
   - **(b) Reduce motion morphs and jumps instead of cross-fading: accept on Android; real fades before iOS (P9).** Memix reads reduce motion as "Remove animations", which is animator duration scale 0. Compose scales every animation by that same setting, so at 0 each one ends on the next frame ([Compose `MotionDurationScale`](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/androidx/compose/ui/MotionDurationScale), Guidance; behavior since Compose 1.2 per a maintainer, [Kotlin Slack](https://slack-chats.kotlinlang.org/t/505584/in-latest-compose-the-animation-duration-scale-now-affects-t)). On Android the 120 ms morph, the jumps and any 120 ms fade all look the same: an instant change, with nothing moving, which is the point. iOS's Reduce Motion doesn't shorten animations, so there it must be the real fade. DESIGN_SYSTEM → Motion and the spec's Motion table now say so.
   - **(c) Extending the last clip stops at the screen edge: follow-up (P8),** not a must-fix. The rule is in the spec.
5. **The rest:**
   - **Icons pass the grammar.** All five sit on the 24 grid inside 2–22, with 2 dp butt-capped, mitred strokes and 1 dp corner radii on rectangles, single-color, tinted per tone.
     - Edit: scissor blades with the finger loops as the solid core.
     - Split: two outlined clips around a solid bar.
     - Delete: lid and handle strokes over a solid can.
     - Duplicate: an open back outline with a 2 dp gap behind a solid front clip.
     - Reorder: an arc with two solid arrowheads, like undo's, over two outlined clips.
     - Each reads at 24 dp in render 51, and none needs its label to be told apart from the others.
   - **Delete in red: yes, there's a token, and it fits.**
     - `danger` `#F6574C` gives 5.2:1 on `surface` (computed). It's a status color, not a second accent: `danger` marks only destructive actions and recording, never a main action (DESIGN_SYSTEM → Color, now explicit). Material 3's error role and Apple's destructive button role work the same way (Guidance: [Material 3 color roles](https://m3.material.io/styles/color/roles), [HIG Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons)).
     - While pressed, on the `surface-raised` fill, it's 4.3:1. Accepted, because it lasts only while the finger is down; the spec notes it.
   - **Hindi at 200%:** passes (see Matches), with P5 for longer labels later.
   - **v2 rules and tokens:** pass.
   - **Accessibility:** passes, apart from P6.
