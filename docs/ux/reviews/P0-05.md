# Design review · P0-05 Bottom navigation and browse screens

**Verdict: Approved with polish** (7 Oct 2026). Reviewed from `docs/ux/reviews/P0-05/` (Pixel_9 emulator at 411 dp and 360 dp, English and Hindi at 200% font scale; iPhone 17 simulator) against `docs/ux/specs/P0-05-navigation-and-browse-screens.md`.

## Matches the spec
- One blue action per screen: the Create block on every tab, plus the Video meme card on Home and the Video meme row in the Create sheet. Selection stays white or `text`, never blue.
- Nothing invented: no trending rows, no sample sounds or templates; the region is a label, not a pill that does nothing; the gallery-permission line is gone.
- Empty states are the focal point of their screens and say why they're empty; Drafts offers the action that fills it.
- The Create sheet covers the nav, closes five ways, and "Start from a template" lands on Templates.
- 360 dp: entry-card titles wrap to two lines and the cards keep equal height; nothing overflows.
- Hindi at 200%: nothing clips; the nav labels fit.

## Polish
1. The nav bar disappears for the length of the pop animation when returning from an editor placeholder (visible on iOS edge-swipe). Minor; revisit when the real editors land (P1), since they also hide the nav.
2. Home is sparse below the entry cards until the trending rows exist (P3-01, P4-05). That's honest for now; not a defect.

## Proposal for the owner
- **Devanagari display face.** Anton and Space Grotesk have no Devanagari, so Hindi falls back to the system font and the headlines lose the Anton voice (see `android-home-hi-200.png`). Option: add an OFL face with Latin and Devanagari in a condensed display style (for example Teko) as the `display` fallback for Hindi only. Needs the owner's OK because it changes the brand type set; until then, the system fallback stays.
