## Must fix before end-of-phase QA
**M1. The caption layer draws outside the preview stage.**
- **Where:** `VideoEditorScreen.kt` → `PreviewStage`, the `BoxWithConstraints` that holds the stage, the `CaptionLayer` and the stage messages.
- **The problem:**
  - Compose doesn't clip children to their parent unless told to. The selection frame (`SelectionFrame`, `TypingStandIn`) is drawn with `drawBehind` on a `matchParentSize` spacer, from the caption's real bounds. When those bounds run past the stage, the white-and-black lines are drawn over whatever is there.
  - Render 167 shows it: a 230-character caption at the top slot draws its frame up over the top bar, through the Close icon. The same happens at the bottom: a caption at the bottom slot, moved to an edge or scaled up draws over the transport row (timecode, play) and, while typing, over the entry field the user is reading. On a 360 × 640 phone the typing stage is only 168 dp tall, so a two-line caption is enough.
  - It breaks the spec's "drawn in the stage's coordinate space, so it may extend past the canvas edge onto the black stage" (the stage is the limit), and "the stage … never covers the field" (Accessibility → 200%). The TalkBack focus rectangles of `CaptionNodes` overflow the same way.
- **Fix:** add `.clipToBounds()` to that `BoxWithConstraints`'s modifier, before `.background(MemixColors.stage)`. Everything the caption layer draws is then cut at the stage edge. The handle already stays inside the stage (`handleCenterOf`), and the stage messages sit inside it, so nothing else changes.
- **Check:** type 200 characters at the top slot (and on 360 × 640): the frame stops at the stage's top and bottom edges, the Close button and the entry field stay clean. Then drag a caption to the bottom edge: nothing draws over the transport row.
- **Spec line:** The selected text on the preview → "Clipped to the stage" (new); QA compares 1.

## Polish
**P1. Style rows scroll edge to edge** (the engineer's limit 2).
- **Where:** `captions/TextPanels.kt` → `ChoiceRow`, the `Row` with `horizontalScroll`.
- **The problem:** the rows scroll inside the ToolPanel's `space-4` padding, so tiles and swatches are cut off with a hard edge 16 dp before the screen edge (renders 162, 172: the Handwritten tile, Hindi "गो…"). A cut in mid-air reads as a layout bug and shows less of the next item, which is the hint that the row scrolls.
- **Fix:** let the row reach past the panel's side padding and move that padding inside the scroll:
  ```kotlin
  Modifier
      .layout { measurable, constraints ->
          val bleed = MemixSpacing.space4.roundToPx()
          val wide = constraints.maxWidth + 2 * bleed
          val placeable = measurable.measure(constraints.copy(minWidth = wide, maxWidth = wide))
          layout(constraints.maxWidth, placeable.height) { placeable.place(-bleed, 0) }
      }
      .horizontalScroll(rememberScrollState())
      .padding(horizontal = MemixSpacing.space4)
      .selectableGroup()
      .semantics { contentDescription = heading }
  ```
  At rest the first tile still lines up with the heading; scrolled items slide out at the screen edge. The panel's own window-inset padding is unaffected (it's on the bottom only).
- **Spec line:** Style panel ("scrolling edge to edge"); ToolPanel README → Content (now explicit).

**P2. The `style` icon's "A" and "a" fuse.**
- **Where:** `:core:designsystem` `composeResources/drawable/ic_style.xml`, the first path.
- **The problem:** the stroked "A"'s right leg comes within about 0.35 px of the solid "a" (at y 18 the leg's outer edge is at x 13.8, the bowl at 14.2). The set's rule is at least 1.4 px between solid parts, or they merge at 2× density. In render 161 the two letters already touch at 3×.
- **Fix:** narrow the "A" by moving its right leg in; the left leg, the "a" and the visual weight stay:
  - from `M2.5,20 L8,5 L13.5,20 M4.8,14.5 H11.2`
  - to `M2.5,20 L7.25,5 L12,20 M4.5,14.5 H10`
  - The nearest gap becomes 1.76 px (at y 18), and the crossbar keeps its 0.28 px inset from the legs.
- **Spec line:** DESIGN_SYSTEM → Iconography (gap rule; `style` now listed).

**P3. A Move or Center step is silent for screen readers.**
- **Where:** `CaptionControl.step`. Size and rotation steps announce `a11y_text_transformed`; Move and Center announce nothing.
- **The problem:** a TalkBack user tapping "Move up" hears no result unless the text hits the edge. The spec asked for "the new position" but gave no string; that gap was mine.
- **Fix:** after a committed Move tap, a hold's release and Center, show a new spoken-only `ToastMessage` (like `TextTransformed`) with the new string `a11y_text_position` = "%1$d% across, %2$d% down" (the caption's center, `centerX` and `centerY` × 100, rounded). Machine-draft id, es, pt and hi as usual; the translator note is in the spec. Not on a limit (the explanation is spoken instead), and not during a hold.
- **Spec line:** Accessibility → Panels; Copy (`a11y_text_position`, new).

## Decisions on the engineer's known limits
1. **Playhead jumps instead of the 200 ms scroll: accepted.** The Style and Position panels cover the timeline as they open, and typing hides it under the keyboard, so the scroll would play where nobody sees it. The preview frame and the timecode already show the move. The spec now says it jumps (Entry and exit; Motion).
2. **Style rows scroll inside the padding: polish P1.** Small and contained, but the spec asked for edge to edge, and the hard cut reads as a bug.
3. **Value bubble without a shadow: accepted.** It matches the timeline bubble (P1-06 review). The readout has 12.4:1 inside its pill, and the pill's edge against the video doesn't need contrast because it's a status readout, not a control. Spec updated (`shadow-float` removed).
4. **Color emoji may get the outline pass: accepted as built, pending the phone.** Android draws color emoji glyphs as images whatever the paint's style, so the stroke pass most likely draws each emoji twice in the same place with nothing visible. At QA, the owner's `mixed-scripts-*` screenshots with outline decide: a dark ring, halo, tint or doubled edge around 😂 is a must-fix before the gate (skip emoji clusters in the stroke pass); a clean emoji closes it. Spec → Fonts, build note.
5. **Teko's size factor: provisional 1.0, set at phase-end QA, before the Phase 1 gate.** From `mixed-scripts-anton` at 100%: compare Anton's cap height ("B") with Teko's headline height (the शिरोरेखा over "ज") on the same line. Within 5%: keep 1.0. Otherwise factor = Anton cap height ÷ Teko headline height, rounded to 0.05, applied to Devanagari runs under Classic only. It must land before the gate because it's part of every draft's look: until the first internal-testing build only the owner's test drafts exist, so no migration is needed. Recorded as `caption.geometry.devanagariSizeFactorUnderClassic` in both token files and in the spec (Fonts).

The other two limits in the commit message are accepted as well:
- **The typing cap needs the stage height from before typing.** Typing always starts from the normal layout, so this holds. It's also why renders 165–168 show a large frame: they start in typing.
- **iOS draws no captions until P7-04:** that's the plan.

## Notes (no change needed)
- **An explanation toast at the top of the stage can cover a caption at the top slot** for its few seconds (render 169). It's P1-04's toast placement, it's transient, and the value bubble isn't shown at the same time. Accepted.
- **The Center button is half under the Position panel's bottom edge at 360 × 780** (render 163), and the panel scrolls. That's ToolPanel's rule, and the half-visible button shows that there's more. The spec's "fits on 360 × 800" was a close estimate.
- **Center on a centered caption** records nothing (the edit session drops unchanged projects) and says nothing. That's fine: nothing happened.
- **`text_entry_label` is set as the field's `contentDescription`.** On some TalkBack versions a content description on an editable field is read instead of the typed words. It's a device check below, not a change yet: if TalkBack reads only "Your text" and not the words, move the label into the field's semantics as a hint and leave the text alone.

## Icons
All ten use the grammar (24 grid, 2 px butt-capped strokes, one tint), except for P2's gap.
- **`text`:** a solid 16 × 3.5 crossbar (the core) over a stroked stem. It reads as "T" at 24 dp (render 160).
- **`edit_text`:** a smaller "T" with an I-beam cursor 2 px to its right. Distinct from `text` at a glance (render 161).
- **`style`:** a stroked "A" with a solid "a". Fails the 1.4 px gap (P2).
- **`position`:** a solid 5 × 5 center square with four strokes to solid arrowheads. Distinct from `reorder` and `move_here`.
- **`minus`, `plus`:** plain 14 px strokes, no core, like `close`. Right for stepper glyphs.
- **`rotate_left`, `rotate_right`:** one arc with a solid head; left is the mirror. They read as counterclockwise and clockwise (render 163).
- **`arrow`:** a shaft into a solid head, turned four ways by rotation. Tips at 20 and the tail at 4 keep the turned copies centered.
- **`scale_rotate`:** a curved stroke with solid heads at both ends, at `icon-small` on the white handle. No core is fine for a handle glyph.
