## Matches the spec
- **Toggle** (renders 110 to 114, 126):
  - A `track-height-video` square in `surface-raised` with `radius-sm`, in a 48 dp target. Measured: its right edge is 8 dp (`space-2`) before 0:00.
  - Sound on: `sounds` in `text-secondary`. Muted: `volume_off` in `text`. The two states differ by shape, not only by brightness (WCAG 1.4.1).
  - Pressed: `hairline` through `pressedColor`, 120 ms. Keyboard focus ring on the square (render 110-tapped).
  - It scrolls with time and with the lanes' vertical scroll, and it's placed in the offset lambda, so playback doesn't recompose it.
  - Hidden in reorder mode (render 113) and when no main clip plays its own sound (render 114, all photos).
  - A `Role.Switch` named "Original audio", 3rd in TalkBack order. "On" means the sound plays.
- **Volume panel** (renders 117, 118, 118b, 124):
  - "This clip" on video clips, 0–200%, with the "Mute original audio of every clip" Toggle row `space-4` below. The row shows the same state as the timeline toggle (render 118: muted, 60% kept).
  - "This sound" on detached audio, with no toggle row, because muting every clip doesn't affect detached audio.
  - The row wraps to two lines in Hindi at 200%, and nothing is cut.
- **Clip tools** (renders 115, 116, 123): Split · Volume · Delete · Duplicate · Detach audio · Reorder · Replace on video clips; photos get neither new tool. Split, Volume and Delete are in view at 360 dp in English and in Hindi at 200%. A video with no sound and an already detached clip keep both tools, and those tools explain on tap (renders 119, 120).
- **Detach audio** (render 122; domain code):
  - The new clip has the source, trim and volume, and starts at the clip's start. It goes on the first Audio lane free for that span, otherwise a new lane, labeled "Original audio" in `track-audio`.
  - The video clip becomes `audioDetached`. The new clip is selected and bonks, with no haptic, as one "Detach audio" step.
  - While muted, the new clip starts at 0% and `explain_detached_muted` shows (renders 121, 127, 128). Otherwise "Detach audio: clip 2" is announced.
  - The bonk follows the P1-16 P5 rule: in render 122-peak the clip swells a few dp at each end and doesn't sweep over the clip before it on the lane.
- **Tokens only:** the new composables use only `MemixColors`, `MemixSize`, `MemixSpacing` and `MemixShapes`. No `Color(0x…)`, raw dp or sp, `FontFamily`, `RoundedCornerShape`, `Brush`, `border`, shadow or v1 hex.
- **v2 rules:**
  - Blue appears on the Meme sounds UI, the slider fill and the "on" Toggle track (the Toggle component's look).
  - White appears only on selection, handles and the playhead.
  - No outlines, gradients or hard shadows.
  - The toggle stays neutral even when muted: it's a setting, not the main action.
- **Contrast** (computed):
  - Toggle icon on: `text-secondary` on `surface-raised` 5.7:1, pressed on `hairline` 4.7:1.
  - Muted: `text` on `surface-raised` 12.5:1.
  - The square's fill is 1.35:1 on `canvas`. That's fine: the icon identifies the control (1.4.11), the same as the Add media tile.
  - Toggle row label `text` on `surface` 13.6:1.
- **Five languages:**
  - All ten keys exist in en, id, es, pt and hi, within budget (`volume_mute_all` es 46 and pt 45 against 48; `explain_detached` es 64 against 70).
  - The "detached" word matches across the tool, the clip's state and both toasts in each language: id "Pisahkan / dipisahkan", es "Separar / separado", pt "Separar / separado", hi "अलग करें / अलग".
  - The single `%` is correct. es uses the no-break space ("0 %") as in P1-16.
- **Analytics** (the engineer's harness): `mute_original` / `unmute_original` once per flip from either place, `detach_audio` once per Detach, `volume` once per release. Nothing for an explanation.

## Must fix before end-of-phase QA
**M1. The toggle sits on the first clip's start handle** (P1-05 → Leading area; my spec had no rule).
- **Where:** `timeline/OriginalAudioToggle.kt`, the early returns at the top of `OriginalAudioToggle`.
- **The problem:** selecting the first main clip puts its start handle at 0:00 (renders 120 and 125). The handle's 48 dp target reaches outward from x0 − 48 dp to 0:00 (`TimelinePainter`'s `startTargetLeft`). The toggle's target covers x0 − 52 to x0 − 4 dp.
  - **Touch:** Compose gives an overlapping touch only to the sibling drawn on top, and the toggle is composed after the canvas. So a touch on most of the start handle toggles the mute and doesn't trim. Trimming the dull start of the first clip is one of the most common meme cuts, and here it silently mutes every clip instead.
  - **Visual:** the white handle and the square touch (renders 120, 125), so the square reads as part of the selection.
- **Fix:** hide the toggle, the same rule as the Add media tile at the other end (P1-16 P2):
  - while the selected main clip starts less than `touch-target` after 0:00. That's always the first clip, and at zoomed-out scales also the clip after a very short first clip. Read the zoom inside `derivedStateOf`, as the tile does.
  - while a trim or move is held.
  - It comes back as soon as the selection clears. Don't shift it left: a target that jumps after a selection tap invites mis-taps.
- **No dead end:** the selected clip's Volume tool holds the same toggle, one tap away. TalkBack loses the switch only while the first clip is selected, and that same tool is next to it.
- **Check:** select the first clip at 0:00 and drag its start handle. It trims, and nothing is muted.
- **Spec line:** P1-05 → Leading area → Hidden (new). Timeline README.

**M2. Detach audio on a clip whose file is missing makes a dead "Original audio" clip** (answer d; my spec had no rule).
- **Where:** `EditorToolBar.kt` → `rememberClipTools`, the `detachTool` in `ClipTools.VIDEO_CLIP`. Only the screen knows a file is missing (`selectedIsMissing`, from the thumbnail reader), and it already passes that in as `isMissing`.
- **The problem:**
  - The copy has gone, so the new green clip points at nothing and plays silence. It still looks like real sound on the timeline.
  - Worse, it outlives the fix. Replace keeps a detached clip's audio and attaches the new video's own sound at 0%, with the toast "The detached sound stays…" (P1-16). So after Detach and then Replace, the user has a silent green clip and the real new sound at 0%. That breaks the one path that fixes a missing clip.
- **Fix:**
  - On a missing clip, Detach audio explains with the existing missing-file toast (`editor_missing_media`): "A clip's file is missing, so it plays black. Replace or delete that clip to fix it."
  - It changes nothing, adds no step and fires no event.
  - No new string is needed: the toast already names Replace, which is first in that bar.
  - Volume keeps working on a missing clip, because Replace keeps the clip's volume.
- **Check:** on `missing-media`, select the missing clip and tap Detach audio. The toast shows and no Audio lane appears. Then Replace, and the clip plays its new sound at its old volume.
- **Spec line:** P1-06 → Detach audio → "A clip whose file is missing" (new). States row.

## Polish
**P1. A duplicate of a detached clip gets its own sound back, at 0%** (answer a).
- **Where:** `:core:domain` `TimelineEdits.kt` → `duplicateItem`, for a main `MediaClip` with `audioDetached`.
- **The problem:** the copy keeps `audioDetached = true`, so its Volume tool says "This clip's sound is on its own track now. Select it there." No detached clip sits under the copy, though. The green clip belongs to the original, at the original's time, so the toast points the user to the wrong place, and the copy's sound can't be brought back except by undo.
- **Fix:**
  - The copy gets `audioDetached = false` and volume 0%.
  - It sounds the same as before (silent), but it's truthful: its Volume opens at 0% and can raise it, and TalkBack reads "Original audio muted" on it.
  - This is the "nothing suddenly gets louder" rule that Replace already uses on a detached clip (P1-16).
  - No toast: if the user deleted the detached clip on purpose, "the detached sound stays" would be wrong.
  - Split doesn't change: both pieces stay detached, because the detached clip still lies under them.
- **Rejected:**
  - Copying the detached audio too: it breaks "independent once detached" and makes two items in one step.
  - Attaching the copy at the clip's volume: it brings back a sound the user removed by deleting the detached clip.
- **Spec line:** P1-06 → Duplicate → "A clip whose sound is detached". Proposals 7 notes the change for the PM's P1-06 check ("keeps every property").

**P2. Tool icons jump 7 dp between a photo clip's tools and a video clip's tools** (answer c).
- **Where:** `:core:designsystem` `component/ToolBar.kt` → `ToolButton`, the label `Text(...)`.
- **The problem:**
  - A one-line tool is 58 dp tall (`space-2` + 24 + `space-1` + 14 + `space-2`); a two-line tool is 72 dp, exactly `toolbar-height`.
  - The bar centers its row, and items sit at the top of it. So a bar with "Detach audio" puts every icon 7 dp higher than a bar with no two-line label (renders 115 and 116: icons at y 1865 against 1885 in display pixels).
  - Switching from a photo clip to a video clip cross-fades two rows of icons 7 dp apart: a double image, and targets that move.
- **Fix:** `minLines = 2` on the label. Every tool is then 72 dp at 100% font, the bar keeps `toolbar-height`, the icon line never moves, and one-line labels sit at the top of their two-line space. At large font sizes the bar still grows as before.
- **Spec line:** ToolBar README → Layout.

**P3. Hindi: one gender for "साउंड"** (machine drafts; P5-06 has the final say).
- The clip state `a11y_state_sound_detached` treats साउंड as masculine ("साउंड अलग किया गया"). The three explanations treat it as feminine ("इस क्लिप की साउंड", "अलग की गई साउंड"). TalkBack users hear both within one task.
- Use the masculine form, the more common one for the loanword, in all three:
  - `explain_detached`: "इस क्लिप का साउंड अब अपने ट्रैक पर है। उसे वहीं चुनें।"
  - `explain_detached_muted`: "मूल ऑडियो म्यूट था, इसलिए अलग किया गया साउंड 0% से शुरू होगा।"
  - `explain_replace_detached`: "अलग किया गया साउंड बना रहेगा, इसलिए इस क्लिप का अपना साउंड 0% से शुरू होगा।"
- **Spec line:** P1-06 → Copy → translator notes.

## Answers to the engineer's questions
- **(a) Duplicate of a detached clip:** don't keep it as is. The copy gets its own sound back at 0% (P1). That's one rule for every new copy of a detached clip's picture (Replace, Duplicate), and its explanation is never wrong.
- **(b) "This sound" on detached audio:** keep "This sound".
  - It's an audio-only item, like a meme sound.
  - Every string about it already calls it a sound ("the detached sound", "This clip's sound is on its own track").
  - "This clip" would make one panel label name two different things, and next to "Mute original audio of every clip" it would suggest the video clip.
- **(c) "Detach audio" on two lines:** fine. Two lines is the budget. It's the fifth tool, so it was never meant to be in view at 360 dp. P2 removes the side effect, the jump.
- **(d) Detach on a missing file:** don't create the clip. The tool explains with the missing-file toast (M2).
- **Your choices:**
  - **Hidden in reorder mode:** confirmed. In render 113 the tiles fill the leading area, and only tiles act in that mode.
  - **No pause on a flip:** confirmed. Hearing the difference at once is the point. That makes the toggle an exception to "touch down on the timeline pauses", now written in P1-05.
  - **No announcement:** confirmed. TalkBack reads the focused switch's new state ("On", "Off"), and an extra "Mute original audio" would repeat it.
  - **Hidden when every clip with sound is detached:** confirmed (`hasOriginalAudio`). Nothing would be left to mute. The spec now says "plays its own sound" instead of "a video with sound".

## Notes (no change needed)
- **A drag that starts on the toggle (or the Add media tile) doesn't scroll the timeline:** the touch belongs to that control. It's one 48 dp square in the empty part of the screen. Accepted.
- **Muted state carries over when the toggle hides.** Mute, then detach every clip: the toggle goes and `Track.muted` stays. When a sound comes back (Replace, P1), the toggle returns showing muted. Truthful, and one tap away.
- **The Volume panel's toggle row sits about 50 dp under the slider track,** not 16. The Slider's own target height is below the track. It still reads as part of the panel; nothing to change now.
- **In render 127, the es pill wraps and lanes scroll at 200%.** That's P1-08 M3's rule working, not a P1-09 issue.
