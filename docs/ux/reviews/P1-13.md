## Matches the spec

**The constants against `design/tokens.json` → `watermark`** (all in `WatermarkCatalog`):

| Property | Token | Code | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text | `Memix` | `TEXT = "Memix"`, drawn as is (no uppercase, no resource) | match |
| Font | `anton_regular.ttf` | `FONT_ID = "anton"` through `typefaceOf` | match |
| Size | 0.04 of the shorter side | `FONT_SIZE_OF_SHORT_SIDE = 0.04f` (43 px at 1080) | match |
| Left margin | 0.03 | `MARGIN_LEFT_OF_SHORT_SIDE = 0.03f` | match, **token fixed** (below) |
| Top margin | 0.03 | `MARGIN_TOP_OF_SHORT_SIDE = 0.03f` | match, **token fixed** (below) |
| Fill | `#FFFFFFCC` | `0xCCFFFFFFL` (0xCC = 80%) | match |
| Shadow | `#00000080`, offset 0, blur 0.15 of font size | `0x80000000L`, `setShadowLayer(size * 0.15f, 0f, 0f, …)` | match |
| Measured to | ink bounds | `getTextBounds`, origin = margin minus the ink's offset (`x = margin - ink.left`, `baseline = margin - ink.top`) | match |

- **Never in the preview.** The only place that builds a `WatermarkOverlay` is `Media3VideoEngine.writeFile` (the export), and only `if (settings.watermark)`. `Media3CompositionBuilder`'s parameter defaults to `null`, so the `CompositionPlayer` preview never gets one.
- **Never in a draft.** `watermark` lives on `ExportSettings` only. `Project` has no field for it, the remembered export choice (resolution, frame rate) doesn't carry it, so no schema bump and an old draft opens the same. The sheet's export builds `ExportSettings` without it (default `true`), so every sheet export has the mark. The only way to turn it off is the debug hook, which is behind `isDebugBuild` in `MainActivity`; a release build can't.
- **On top of everything, whole video.** One composition-level `OverlayEffect(listOfNotNull(captionOverlay, watermarkOverlay))`: overlays draw in list order, so the mark is last and above the captions, and above any sticker P2 adds before it. Composition-level effects run after each clip's `CanvasFraming`, so the input frame is the output frame and `min(canvas.width, canvas.height)` is the output's shorter side at every resolution and shape (the 480p, 1080p, 4K and 16:9 cases in the spec). No animation, no time window.
- **Draw-once is safe.** `onDraw` returns early when the frame size is unchanged. I read Media3's `CanvasOverlay`: it keeps one bitmap and canvas and only reallocates when the size changes, and it never clears between calls, so the mark stays. `CaptionOverlay` relies on the same behavior and was accepted in P1-10.
- **Shadow with no offset, no outline, no hard edge.** `setShadowLayer(radius, 0, 0, color)`, no stroke pass, no gradient. The v1 look is not back.
- **The row** (`WatermarkRow`): `radius-md`, `surface-raised` (one step above the sheet's `surface`; the segmented track beside it is `canvas`, so it reads as a note card and not as a control), `space-3` padding, full width, no `clickable`, so no ripple or press state. Title `bodyStrong` in `text` (default), line `label` in `text-secondary`; both wrap, no `maxLines`, nothing is cut. Order in the sheet: settings, row, facts line, notices, Export video. Tokens only: `MemixShapes.radiusMd`, `MemixColors.surfaceRaised`, `MemixSpacing.space3` and `space1`, `MemixTheme.type`. No blue, no white, no outline: the row adds no second action.
- **Contrast of the row** (recomputed from tokens): `text` on `surface-raised` 12.4:1, `text-secondary` (`#A6A6A6`) on `#2C2C2C` 5.7:1. Both pass AA at 13 sp.
- **Spoken text:** one node, "Memix watermark. Small text in the top-left corner of your video.", as the spec says (`clearAndSetSemantics`), no click action.
- **Copy, five languages.** Both keys exist in all five and "Memix" is kept in every title:

  | Key | en | id | es | pt | hi | Budget |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `export_watermark_title` | Memix watermark (15) | Watermark Memix (15) | Marca de agua de Memix (22) | Marca d'água do Memix (21) | Memix वॉटरमार्क (14) | 24 |
  | `export_watermark_body` | Small text in the top-left corner of your video (47) | Teks kecil di pojok kiri atas videomu (37) | Texto pequeño en la esquina superior izquierda de tu video (58) | Texto pequeno no canto superior esquerdo do seu vídeo (53) | आपके वीडियो के ऊपरी बाएँ कोने में छोटा टेक्स्ट (45) | 60 |

  The words follow each language's editors ("watermark" in id, "marca de agua" in es, "marca d'água" in pt, "वॉटरमार्क" in hi). "Videomu" matches the other Indonesian strings ("Videomu perlu setidaknya satu klip"). The unescaped apostrophe in `d'água` is the project's convention (the English file does the same with "can't"). No emoji, sentence case, "your" form.
- **v2 rules:** no outline around the row, no hard shadow, no gradient, no v1 value, one blue action per surface unchanged (the row has none).

## Polish

**P1. Make the white-frame check a pass or fail, with the fix decided in advance (device check).**
- **Where:** `WatermarkCatalog.FILL_ARGB`, `SHADOW_ARGB`, `SHADOW_BLUR_OF_FONT_SIZE`.
- **The problem:** on a white frame the fill (white at 80%) is white, so the mark is carried only by the shadow. Computed from the build's numbers: Anton at 43 px has stems about 8 px wide, the blur radius is 6.5 px (sigma about 4 px), so the halo next to a stem peaks at about 25 to 30% black, around sRGB 180 to 190. That is about 1.9:1 against the white fill (Assumption, from the blur math; the frame decides). A logotype has no WCAG ratio (SC 1.4.3), so this is our own bar, not a requirement. If Android multiplies the shadow's alpha by the paint's alpha (I believe it does, not confirmed here), the shadow is 40%, not 50%, and the halo is weaker still.
- **Our bar** (Assumption): on the white reference frame the letters can be read at arm's length on a phone at 100% zoom, and the darkest halo pixel next to the ink is at or below sRGB 175.
- **If it fails:** one constant at a time, no draft or schema change, only new exports: first `SHADOW_ARGB` to `0x99000000` (60%), then `SHADOW_BLUR_OF_FONT_SIZE` to 0.20. Don't touch the fill (the 80% white is the "quiet credit" look) and don't add an outline. Tell me which frame failed and I'll update `design/tokens.json` first.
- **Spec line:** The mark → Legibility; QA compares 3.

**P2. A debug catalog label contradicts the spec.**
- **Where:** `core/designsystem/.../catalog/ComponentCatalog.kt` line 277: `Toggle("Show watermark preview", on, { on = it })`.
- **The problem:** the spec and the Toggle README say there is no watermark preview or setting. A debug-only sample label, but it is the kind of line that gets copied into a real screen.
- **Fix:** use the README's own example: `Toggle("Mute original audio", on, { on = it })`. No other change.

**P3. The row's spoken text hard-codes the full stops.**
- **Where:** `ExportSheet.kt` → `WatermarkRow` (`contentDescription = "$title. $body."`).
- **The problem:** it adds a Latin full stop after Hindi text that ends without one in the string (Hindi's own stop is "।"). TalkBack handles it, so this is minor.
- **Fix, optional:** replace `clearAndSetSemantics { … }` with `semantics(mergeDescendants = true) {}`: TalkBack reads the title, then the line, with each language's own pauses, and it's still one non-clickable node. Same on `FactsLine` if the P1-12 P3 fix is made there. Device check: TalkBack reads it as one item in all five languages.
- **Spec line:** Accessibility (first bullet).

**P4. QA's ink measurement should use the tallest glyph, not the "M".**
- **Where:** `docs/qa/phase-1/engineer-hand-checks.md` → P1-13 B1 ("the cap M about 31 px tall").
- **The problem:** `getTextBounds` returns the box of everything drawn, so the top margin is measured to the highest ink among M, e, m, i, x. In most faces the dot of the "i" can sit at or a little above the capital's top, and the cap-height figure (about 31 px) is my estimate for Anton, not a measurement.
- **Fix:** measure the ink box of the whole word (the tallest pixel row of any letter) and write the measured heights into the reference README. Pass: left and top edges 32 px ± 2 px; the "M" and the "i" dot within 2 px of each other is fine either way. If the engineer prefers, drop the "31 px" line.
- **Spec line:** Measured.

## The owner's TikTok and Reels test (phase-end QA, about 10 minutes)
You can run this yourself, no one else needed. The mark is in the file, so the test is only about whether an app's own buttons cover it.
1. Export one 9:16 1080p meme from the app (the sheet's default). Make sure its first seconds have some picture in the top-left, not black.
2. Post it privately: TikTok ("Only me") and Instagram Reels (a private or "Close friends" post, or the draft preview). Play it in each app's own full-screen feed.
3. Look at the top-left corner on a phone, as a viewer would. Write down for each app: fully visible, partly covered, or hidden.
4. **Decision rule** (Assumption, ours): if the mark is fully visible in both, keep A (3%). If it is partly or fully covered in either, choose B (top margin about 13%, `MARGIN_TOP_OF_SHORT_SIDE = 0.13f`), then check B against a top caption in the first caption slot (25% of the height) so the two don't touch.
5. Send the PM the result and the two screenshots. Attach them to `docs/ux/reviews/P1-13/` as `tiktok-test` and `reels-test`.
