# Design review: P2-14 (sticker picker, My stickers)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 14 renders `98-*` (desktop, generated stand-in stickers: a square, a 3:1 wide, a 1:3 tall and a tiny one), the spec, the handoff and the `ic_sticker` drawable. Nothing ran on a device, so the real thumbnails, the copy time, the long-press feel and TalkBack are for the device pass.

## Verdict: Approved with polish

## What works
- **The sheet** (`98-stickers-sheet`, `-360x640`): title "Stickers", a close button, a three-column grid of 104 dp tiles on `surface-raised` with `space-2` gaps, newest first, the sheet at about 70% of the window so the canvas stays visible above it. Stickers of every shape (square, wide, tall, tiny) fit inside their tiles with air around them and no checkerboard; the 3:1 and 1:3 ones are centered, not stretched. Without catalog packs the tab row is hidden, as specified.
- **The empty state** (`empty-with-photo`, `empty-no-photo`): the `sticker` icon, "No stickers yet", the body line and one action. With a photo on the canvas the one blue is Remove background; without one it is a Secondary Add photo, so the sheet never offers a blue that can't act. Hindi at 200% wraps and fits with the action in view.
- **Delete:** a long-press Menu with one item, a toast "Sticker deleted" with a blue Undo, and the tile gone at once.
- **Landing** (`sticker-landed`): a 3:1 sticker on the canvas, centered, selected with the two-tone frame and the corner handle, at 35% of the shorter side; the selected-layer bar replaces the main bar (Position, Opacity, Delete, Duplicate, Lock, ...).
- **The main bar** order is now Formats, Text, Stickers, Add photo, Draw, Edit photo, ...: the final order from P2-05.
- **The missing file** (`missing-tile`): the tile shows the `photo` icon on `surface-raised`, a tap says "This sticker's file is missing", and Delete still works: no dead end.
- **The no-space case** keeps the sheet open with a toast (P2-14's rule).
- **`ic_sticker`: accepted.** A square with a solid folded corner reads as a sticker with a peeled corner, in the 2 dp stroke grammar, distinct from `duplicate` (two squares) and `photo`. It is clear in the main bar next to Text and Add photo. No change.

## Must fix
None.

## Polish
- **P1. The 300 ms pressed look is not built** (known gap). While a large sticker copies into the project, a second tap does nothing and nothing shows why. After 300 ms the tile should take the `hairline` pressed fill and stay so until the sheet closes (spec: Interactions). Taps are ignored meanwhile, which is right.
- **P2. Focus after a sticker lands** (known gap). The sheet closes and TalkBack's focus falls back to the editor top bar. The spoken confirmation `a11y_sticker_added` is the essential part and is announced; moving focus to the selected-layer bar is better. Check on the device, and if TalkBack lands somewhere confusing, request focus on the bar's first item.
- **P3. The Undo toast is narrow and sits over the top bar** (`98-stickers-deleted-undo`): a small box in the middle with "Undo" on a second line, while the missing-file toast is full width. Toasts should share one width rule (the Toast component's): full width with `space-4` margins, the action at the end of the same line when it fits. Keep it at the top while a sheet is open (the sheet owns the bottom), as built.
- **P4. Missing-file copy:** "This sticker's file is missing. Delete it from the list." doesn't say how. Change to "This sticker's file is missing. Touch and hold it to delete it." (budget 70).
- **P5. The pending delete is dropped if the editor is torn down inside the 8 seconds** (builder's note: the file stays). Acceptable, since nothing is lost from the user's side; but then the sticker reappears in the list the next time. Say so in the handoff, or finish the delete on teardown.
- **P6. Many stickers:** the grid is lazy, but the sheet's thumbnail decode (128 px, one per file) is unchecked at hundreds of stickers. QA scrolls a list of about 200 and checks smoothness.
