- **Landing** (`40-text-selected-bar`, `40-typing-new`): the first caption lands at the top slot (center `y` 0.25, measured 0.249), centered, at 100%, selected with the two-tone frame and the handle at the bottom-right; the empty landing square while typing is 54 dp (48 dp or more) at the same spot.
- **Selected-text bar** (`40-text-selected-bar`, `-360x640`): Edit text · Style · Position · **Delete fully in view, fourth** · Opacity (cut, the scroll cue). A locked text starts with **Unlock**, with the lock badge in the handle's place (`40-text-locked-selected`); a locked sticker the same (`40-sticker-locked-selected`).
- **Typing** (`40-typing-new`, `40-typing-edit`): the entry bar replaces the tool bar (field, Ghost Done); Undo, Redo and the ratio pill are drawn disabled.
- **Style panel** (`40-style-panel-*`): five FontTiles (Classic white), the ten color swatches, the Outline row; the canvas refits above it. **Position and Opacity panels** carry the text ladder (100% shown) and the layer panels.
- **Layers rows** (`40-layers-panel-text-and-stickers`, `-sticker-rows-top`): a text row names its **first line** in one line with an ellipsis ("WHEN THE BUILD P…"), the `text` icon on a darker `surface` square (5.7:1); sticker rows read "Sticker" with the picture fitted inside on `surface`; "File missing" shows the `photo` icon; "Locked" and the faded and hidden states are in the meta line. - **Copy:** all P2-03 keys exist in five languages and match the tool labels they name ("Texto"/"Añadir foto", "टेक्स्ट"/"फ़ोटो जोड़ें", "Teks"/"Tambah foto", "Texto"/"Adicionar foto"). Sticker is "Sticker", "Stiker", "Figurinha", "स्टिकर".

## Must fix before end-of-phase QA

**M1. The empty-canvas card's background is cut to the canvas frame at 200% font on a 9:16 canvas, and its text runs outside it.**
- **Where:** `PhotoStage.kt` → `EmptyCanvasCard` and where it is placed (line 203).
- **The problem:** at 360 × 640 and 200% font on a 9:16 canvas, the card's dark background is **253 dp wide, as wide as the frame, with square corners** (`40-hindi-200-empty-blank`, `40-spanish-200-empty-blank`), while the title and body run up to **271 dp** and spill onto the black stage on both sides ("Este lienzo está" starts left of the background). The spec centers the card on the **stage**, `space-4` in from the stage's sides, so the background is about 328 dp wide with `radius-lg` corners, "because a 9:16 frame's width cut the Hindi body at 200%" (P1-04's lesson, which the code's own comment cites). At 100% font on a 9:16 canvas on 360 × 800 the two widths coincide (328 dp), so the English renders look right. The same card on a 1:1 canvas is fine (P2-01 `08-hindi-200-empty`), which is why this was not caught before.
- **Fix:** draw the card in the stage's layer, above and outside the frame's clip, so its width is the stage's minus `space-4` on each side. Then re-render the two 200% frames and a 3:4 or 9:16 canvas at 100%. If the cause is a clip on the scene layer, move the card out of it; if it is the width constraint, pass the stage's width, not the frame's.
- **Spec line:** States → "Empty canvas card" ("Centered on the stage (not the frame)", "`space-4` in from the stage's sides", "`scrim` fill, `radius-lg`").

**M2. `canUndo` is a wrong stand-in for "has history": a blank canvas where only the ratio or color was changed shows the Undo wording.**
- **Where:** `PhotoStage.kt` line 203 (`hasHistory = state.canUndo`) and `EmptyCanvasCard`; the state in `PhotoEditorUiState.kt`.
- **The problem:** Blank → tap the ratio pill → pick 9:16 is the usual first move, and it is a committed, undoable step (a blank project is saved only at its first layer, but its edits are in the session history, P2-01). After it `canUndo` is true, so the card on a canvas that **never had a layer** reads "Tap Undo to bring your layer back, or add something new…". There is no layer to bring back, and Undo would only undo the ratio. Spec → The empty-canvas card: the Undo body is for "a canvas that was emptied (a layer was deleted)".
- **Fix:** replace the stand-in with a real flag: `hadLayer` in the UI state, set to true the first time `layers` is non-empty during the visit (a reopened draft always starts with layers, since an empty draft is deleted), and `hasHistory = state.hadLayer`. The body choice then follows what the card says. Hand check: Blank, change the ratio and the color: the card keeps "Tap Text to add words…"; add text, delete it: "Tap Undo to bring your layer back…".
- **Spec line:** The empty-canvas card ("A canvas that never had a layer" / "A canvas that was emptied").

## Judgment on the four deviations
1. **The ratio pill is inert while typing in the video editor too: accepted.** The spec asked for it and the PM confirmed it: the typed words aren't in the project, and the canvas must not change under the keyboard. The old behavior (commit, then open the Canvas panel) was P1-11's "Opening from other states" line; I updated that spec line and P1-10's Typing section (below). QA re-runs the video editor's typing and ratio steps (the lifted code is shared).
2. **The typing refit shrinks the stage with the keyboard, no cover animation: accepted.** The spec asked for the frame to refit "frame by frame with the keyboard's own animation, never bigger"; an inset-driven shrink is that. The check is on the device (the stage must follow the keyboard without a jump, the frame never larger than before typing, the field and the caption both visible at 360 × 640).
3. **`canUndo` as "has history": not accepted as built.** See M2.
4. **Focus return after a panel or the picker: accepted** (the known open item from P2-01 review P4). It becomes a must-fix only if TalkBack lands on nothing after Done, or after the Style panel closes.

## Polish (not blocking)
**P1. "Add photo" breaks across lines in the Undo body** (`40-after-delete-empty-card`: "…with Text or Add / photo."). Use a no-break space inside the tool names the sentence points to ("Add photo", "Añadir foto", "Adicionar foto", "Tambah foto", "फ़ोटो जोड़ें") in `photo_empty_body` and `photo_empty_body_blank`, so a tool name never splits.

**P2. Hindi `photo_empty_body` reads awkwardly** ("…या टेक्स्ट या फ़ोटो जोड़ें से कुछ नया जोड़ें"): the tool names sit in the middle of a "with" phrase. Suggest "…या टेक्स्ट या फ़ोटो जोड़ें दबाकर कुछ नया जोड़ें।" (machine draft until P5; fine to leave, but fix it with the nbsp edit).

**P3. At 200% font, the typing field in Hindi gets only 59% of the bar** (`40-hindi-200-typing`): "हो गया" takes 37% of the width and the four-word caption wraps to four lines, so the entry bar is about a third of the screen. This is the lifted P1-10 bar and behaves the same in video. Not a P2-03 defect; if QA finds it cramped on the device, the fix is Done under the field at 200% in a later pass.

**P4. At 200% Spanish, Delete (fourth) is clipped** (`40-spanish-200-text-selected-bar`: "Elimin…"). The ToolBar rule keeps Delete in view at 100%; at 200% the labels widen and the fourth tool is partly cut. This is the known ToolBar trade-off (tools widen, the bar scrolls); no change now. QA confirms the scroll reaches Delete.

**P5. The Style panel does not fit at 360 × 800, contrary to my spec** (`40-style-panel-360x800`: the Outline row's top peeks at the bottom; at 640 it is not visible at all, there is no peek). The spec said "about 292 dp of content, so at 360 × 800 (cap 320) it fits": the 292 excluded the title and padding. The content is about 340 dp with the title. I changed the spec (the panel scrolls at both heights). The 800 render has a scroll cue (the peek); the 640 render has none: QA checks whether the Outline row is discoverable at 360 × 640; if not, the fix is to let the panel peek a few dp of the Outline heading.

**The M1 change (exact, tokens only).** In `PhotoStage.kt` → `EmptyCanvasCard`, change the fill:
```
.background(MemixColors.scrim)      →      .background(MemixColors.surfaceRaised)
```
Nothing else: the same `radius-lg`, `space-4` padding, `title` and `body` text in `text`. Why `surface-raised`:
- It is opaque, so the card reads as one card over the white canvas, the black stage and a black canvas, and its edges meet the stage's edges instead of fading at the frame's.
- `text` `#F2F2F2` on `#2C2C2C` is **12.4:1** everywhere (the old `scrim` pair was 7.6:1 over a white canvas and undefined over a picture); no new token.
- `surface-raised` is the design system's color for empty frames and raised panels on dark ground (tokens usage: "empty media frames"). The cost is that the card hides the canvas under it completely; the card is a message, so that is right.
- Keep `scrim` for the lock badge and the slot hints (small chips over the user's picture, where seeing the picture through them matters).
- Re-render `40-hindi-200-empty-blank`, `40-spanish-200-empty-blank`, `40-blank-start-empty-card` and one black-canvas frame to check.
I updated the P2-01 spec (States, the card's look and its contrast line) and the P2-03 spec follows P2-01's card.
