## Must fix before end-of-phase QA

**M1. A locked layer's bar must start with Unlock.**
- **Where:** `PhotoEditorScreen.kt` → `selectedLayerTools` (the `tools` list, line 317), and the ToolBar's scroll state.
- **The problem:** while a layer is locked, Position, Opacity and Delete only explain. The built order puts those three first and Unlock fifth. At 360 dp Unlock is cut in half (`04-locked-toast`, `02-locked-selected`); at 200% font in Spanish the bar shows exactly Posición, Opacidad, Eliminar and Unlock is not on screen (`08-spanish-200-selected`). A user who taps the lock badge area or drags the layer gets "Unlock it to change it", and the way to do so is two scrolls away. Principle 5 (errors say what to do next) and Principle 4 (reach).
- **Fix:** I resolved the spec's contradiction in favor of "Unlock first" (the spec now says so, and why). In `selectedLayerTools`:
  ```
  val tools = buildList {
      if (layer.locked) add(lock)            // "Unlock", first
      if (missing) add(delete)               // a locked, missing layer: Unlock, then Delete
      add(position); add(opacity)
      if (!missing) add(delete)
      add(duplicate)
      if (!layer.locked) add(lock)           // "Lock" keeps its usual fifth place
      add(hide); add(layers)
  }
  ```
  Because tapping Lock now reorders the bar under the user's finger, also scroll the ToolBar back to its start (instant, no animation) when `layer.locked` changes, so the new first tool is the one in view. If the ToolBar owns its scroll state, expose a `resetScrollKey` (or key the scroll state on `layer.locked`). The Crossfade does not help here: `hasSelection` does not change.
- **Spec line:** Tool bars → Selected-layer bar → "Lock and Hide swap their label and state" (rewritten 28 Oct); States → "Layer selected, locked".
- **Check:** `photo-layers-10`, select the locked layer, 360 × 800 and es 200%: Unlock is the first tool beside Close layer tools; Lock it and unlock it again: the bar returns to its start both times. TalkBack reads the bar in the same order.

**M2. Two shared strings name the wrong editor.**
- **Where:** `:core:ui` strings `explain_canvas_widest` and `explain_canvas_tallest`, five languages. They shipped with P1-11 (video) and the photo editor reuses them: tapping Custom's + at the limit in a photo says "This is as wide as a video can be." My spec said `canvas_*` keeps its strings, which was my miss.
- **Fix:** neutral wording, one pair of keys for both editors (budget 40; machine drafts until P5). The word "canvas" is each language's existing `canvas_title`:

  | Key | en | id | es | pt | hi |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `explain_canvas_widest` | This is as wide as a canvas can be. | Ini selebar mungkin untuk kanvas. | Un lienzo no puede ser más ancho. | Uma tela não pode ser mais larga. | कैनवास इससे ज़्यादा चौड़ा नहीं हो सकता। |
  | `explain_canvas_tallest` | This is as tall as a canvas can be. | Ini setinggi mungkin untuk kanvas. | Un lienzo no puede ser más alto. | Uma tela não pode ser mais alta. | कैनवास इससे ज़्यादा लंबा नहीं हो सकता। |

  The video editor reads the same keys, so its toast changes too (harmless: it is still true). No other shared string says "video" (`grep` of `:core:ui` and `:feature:photo-editor`).
- **Spec line:** Copy (reused keys; two rows added 28 Oct).

**M3. The Layers panel list needs room for two rows: move the Opacity row into the list when the panel is tight.**
- **Where:** `LayersPanel.kt` → `LayersPanel` (`OpacityRow` above `LayerList`).
- **The problem:** measured from the renders, the header and the fixed Opacity row take 160 dp of the 256 dp panel at 360 × 640, which leaves 96 dp for the list: 1.7 rows with the selected row ringed (`03-layers-panel-360x640`). At 200% font (`08-hindi-200-layers-panel`) the first row starts 199 dp down and the list window is about 40 dp: half a row, no lock or eye button in view, scrolling inside a window the size of a toast. Spec → Accessibility says "nothing is cut"; Principle 6 says it works at 200%. The build matches my spec, so the flaw is the spec's (fixed in the spec now): Opacity has its own panel one tap away, and the list is the Layers panel's job.
- **Fix:** the rule is in the spec (Layers panel → Where the Opacity row lives). In code:
  - **Roomy** (`maxHeight >= 300.dp` and `LocalDensity.current.fontScale < 1.3f`): as built, the Opacity row fixed above the list.
  - **Tight** (otherwise): render `OpacityRow` as the first `item` of the `LazyColumn` (key `"opacity"`, content type `"opacity"`), not above it. The list's reorder, `animateScrollToItem(index)` and row math must add 1 to indexes while the Opacity item exists. The Move buttons stay in the fixed top row.
  - Check: 360 × 640 at 100% shows the header and 3 or more rows; 360 × 640 at 200% in Hindi shows the header and at least one whole row.
- **Spec line:** Layers panel (new paragraph), Accessibility → 200% font.

## Polish (not blocking)

**P1. Keep "40%" and "opacity" together.** `layer_meta_opacity`, five languages. The worst case wraps at 100% on 360 dp and leaves "40%" at the end of a line and "opacity" alone on the next (`09-row-states`, the "Locked · Hidden · 40% opacity" row). Put a no-break space between the number and the word (` ` in the XML; Compose resources decode it). Only the languages that put the number first need it: en and hi ("%1$d% पारदर्शिता"). id, es and pt put the word first ("Opacidad %1$d%"), so a break can only fall after the word; put a no-break space there too, for the same reason.

**P2. One Hindi spelling for "canvas".** `:core:ui` hi has "कैनवस" (`canvas_title`, `canvas_pill_a11y`) and `:feature:photo-editor` hi has "कैनवास" (`photo_empty_title`, `photo_stage_a11y`), so the pill, the panel and the empty card disagree. Use "कैनवास" (the usual spelling) in all of them, and in M2's new strings.

**P3. Custom open pushes Background out of sight, with no hint that it exists** (`05-canvas-panel-photo-start`). A 4:3 camera photo is a Custom canvas, so the usual arrival has Custom selected and the steppers open: title, Ratio and the two steppers fill the 320 dp panel at 360 × 800, and the Background row and the hint line are below the fold with no peeking edge. The spec accepted a scrolling panel; the render shows the cost. Not a must-fix because Background rarely matters under a full-bleed photo. Two cheap options for the engineer, in order of preference:
  - **(a)** If the swatch row's top edge can peek (a 12 dp sliver of the next row), nothing else is needed. Try it first.
  - **(b)** Otherwise cap the Canvas panel at 45% while Custom is open.
  QA decides on the device (item 4 below). If neither is wanted, I will move Background above the steppers in a later pass.

**P4. Focus does not return to the tool after a panel closes** (builder deviation 8): accepted for P2-01 as a follow-up, not a must-fix. The video editor has the same gap (P1-02 and P1-14 reviews). A TalkBack user lands on the top bar after Done, one swipe or two from where they were. Rule for QA (same as P1-14): it becomes a must-fix if TalkBack lands on nothing or the status bar after Done. The fix is one shared helper (`requestFocus` on the tool that opened the panel, after the exit animation starts), best done once for all editors; I suggest the PM adds it to a later P2 ticket or the P2 polish pass.

**P5. Render the hint line.** `photo_background_hidden` is below the fold in the only photo-start render, so it was never seen. Add a render with the Canvas panel scrolled to the bottom (or taller) for the 4:3 start. QA checks it on the device anyway.

## Answers to the builder's questions

**1. Locked layer's Unlock: move it to the front?** Yes (M1). The spec's two lines disagreed; "Unlock first" is right. Reasons: the three tools in front of it all refuse while locked, it is the one tool that fixes the situation the toast just described, it is off screen at 360 dp and invisible at 200% es, and the cost is one rule. Lock keeps its usual place when the layer is unlocked, so nobody's habit moves. The bar scrolls back to its start when Lock or Unlock reorders it. I changed the spec.

**2. The 14 dp eye icon height: acceptable.** The outline's centerline sits ±6 from the middle (control points at y 0 and 24), so the outside is 14 px tall. At 12 px outside the inner edge would be 4 px from the middle and the pupil's edge 3 px: 1 px of air, under the 1.4 px grammar rule. 14 px keeps the 2 px air and reads as an eye at `icon-small` and `icon` size in the renders (09). The pointed ends make it about 21 px wide with the miter, close to the spec's 20. DESIGN_SYSTEM now says 20 × 14 and why.

## Judgment on the other deviations
- **Row thumbnails from the scene decoder at about 128 px: accepted.** The thumbnail reader returns square center crops, and the spec's row wants the picture fitted inside (the 4:3 thumbnails in 09 show the whole picture, 40 × 30). Cost is fine: one 128 px bitmap per file (about 64 KB), requested only when a row composes, cached for the editor's life and released at close (`LayerThumbnails`). 128 px is below the 192 px ceiling. The spec's request 6 is updated. Device check: scroll the 11-row list quickly and watch for pop-in and for memory (QA item 7).
- **Reused strings that say "video": a must-fix** (M2), but only two strings are affected.
- **About one row at 200% font on 360 × 640: not acceptable as built**, because the Opacity row is fixed and the list gets 40 dp (M3). The scrolling itself is fine.
- **Focus return not built: accepted as P4.**
