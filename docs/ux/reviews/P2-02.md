# Design review: P2-02 part a (Plain, Classic, Caption bar, Demotivational)

Reviewed 5 Nov 2026 from the 19 desktop renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P2-02/` (text is a stand-in; no emulator), the spec, and the handoff. Panels and Bubbles are 02b: only their model, drawing and references exist, and no tile or tool shows for them (correct: a tile never shows before its format works).

## Verdict: Approved with polish

Nothing breaks the spec. One polish item (P1) is a spec amendment from this review and should be applied before 02a is committed. Items for 02b are recorded so they don't wait for another review.

## What works
- **Formats panel:** Plain selected in white (selection), tiles with icon and label, horizontal scroll with the next tile cut at the edge as the cue; the selected tile scrolls into view (Spanish and Hindi at 200%). The hint line appears only when the photo doesn't fill the area (tall-canvas Demotivational, Caption bar) and is absent on a fresh Plain start.
- **Main bar:** Formats first; at 200% (es, hi) labels wrap, icons stay aligned, nothing is cut.
- **Slot hints:** Classic and Caption bar read clearly (`scrim` over the photo, gray over the white bar, white text 8:1 or more); they hide while a text layer is selected (`50-classic-hints-hidden-while-selected`); the filled states show the Serif title and Modern subtitle defaults as specified.
- **Placement:** the photo fits inside what the format leaves (bar, poster frame) and the canvas ratio never changes (owner's answer).

## Builder's points
1. **`scrim` hint over the Demotivational poster: yes, fix it.** Over black, `scrim` disappears and only the label shows, so the tap target's extent can't be seen. Use `surface-raised` for hints that sit on the poster; keep `scrim` over photos and the white bar (an opaque gray block would hide the photo). Applied to the spec (slot hint paragraph); contrast `text` on `surface-raised` 12.4:1. This is P1 below, not a builder defect.
2. **Thought-bubble dots (02b): yes, and a rule rather than just a longer default.** At the spec radii the three dots measure 0.16 + 0.104 + 0.064 = 0.328 S before gaps, so a tail shorter than about 0.40 S can't show them. Rule: the dot chain scales down with the tail's reach (reach divided by 0.40 S, floor 40% of the radii); under the floor, draw two dots, then one. Default thought tail reach 0.45 S from the body edge, clamped to the canvas. Same renderer in editor, export and references.
3. **Panels edge cells (02b): the model fix is right; the rule is equal cells at even.** The spec says a gutter between cells and the same margin around (line 91), which means cells are equal when the dividers are "even". Dividers centered on a gutter with margin = gutter leave edge cells half a gutter wider. Define the even position per divider as the one that makes the cell sizes equal: x_k = m + k·c + (k − 0.5)·g with c = (1 − 2m − (n − 1)g) / n for n equal cells; snap and reset use it. At 1080 px the difference is 6 px, but "snap to even" must produce equal cells or users will see it in a 2 × 3 grid. A reference frame for a 3-column layout at even.
4. **Bubble scale 25 to 500%: accept.** Same as text. Two checks for 02b: at 25% the tail handle and the scale handle stay 48 dp targets (handles don't shrink with the bubble), and at 500% the bubble may leave the canvas (layers clip at the frame, P2-01).

## Fixes
**Must fix:** none.

**Polish**
- **P1 (before commit):** the slot hint fill over the Demotivational poster is `surface-raised` (above). Spec: P2-02 slot hint paragraph, amended.
- **P2:** at 200% Hindi (`50-hindi-200-classic-hints`) the two-line hint grows to about 80 dp and the bottom one runs to the canvas edge, where it looks cut; it also covers the photo's own caption. Keep every hint fully inside the frame (shift it in; don't crop it) and cap it at two lines. Spec line: the hint is "never wider than 90% of S" and sits in the slot; add "and inside the frame".
- **P3:** at 200% the Formats panel's hint line is cut mid-line at the panel's bottom (`50-spanish-200-formats-panel`, `50-hindi-200-formats-panel`). The panel is meant to scroll (40% cap); confirm on the device that it does and that the last line can be fully reached; a half-visible line with no scroll is a defect.
- **P4:** a README note, not a fix: the renders show the desktop stand-in font; the real fonts (Rozha, Poppins, Anton) and Hindi in Teko need the device check at phase QA.

## For the device pass (QA)
