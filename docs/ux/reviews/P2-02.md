# Design review: P2-02 part a (Plain, Classic, Caption bar, Demotivational)

Reviewed 5 Nov 2026 from the 19 desktop renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P2-02/` (text is a stand-in; no emulator), the spec, and the handoff. Panels and Bubbles are 02b: only their model, drawing and references exist, and no tile or tool shows for them (correct: a tile never shows before its format works).

## Verdict: Approved with polish

Nothing breaks the spec. One polish item (P1) is a spec amendment from this review and should be applied before 02a is committed. Items for 02b are recorded so they don't wait for another review.

## What works
- **Formats panel:** Plain selected in white (selection), tiles with icon and label, horizontal scroll with the next tile cut at the edge as the cue; the selected tile scrolls into view (Spanish and Hindi at 200%). The hint line appears only when the photo doesn't fill the area (tall-canvas Demotivational, Caption bar) and is absent on a fresh Plain start.
- **Main bar:** Formats first; at 200% (es, hi) labels wrap, icons stay aligned, nothing is cut.
- **Slot hints:** Classic and Caption bar read clearly (`scrim` over the photo, gray over the white bar, white text 8:1 or more); they hide while a text layer is selected (`50-classic-hints-hidden-while-selected`); the filled states show the Serif title and Modern subtitle defaults as specified.
- **Placement:** the photo fits inside what the format leaves (bar, poster frame) and the canvas ratio never changes (owner's answer).

## Builder's points
1. **`scrim` hint over the Demotivational poster: yes, fix it.** Over black, `scrim` disappears and only the label shows, so the tap target's extent can't be seen. Use `surface-raised` for hints that sit on the poster; keep `scrim` over photos and the white bar (an opaque gray block would hide the photo). Applied to the spec (slot hint paragraph); contrast `text` on `surface-raised` 12.4:1. This is P1 below, not a builder defect.
2. **Thought-bubble dots (02b): yes, and a rule rather than just a longer default.** At the spec radii the three dots measure 0.16 + 0.104 + 0.064 = 0.328 S before gaps, so a tail shorter than about 0.40 S can't show them. Rule: the dot chain scales down with the tail's reach (reach divided by 0.40 S, floor 40% of the radii); under the floor, draw two dots, then one. Default thought tail reach 0.45 S from the body edge, clamped to the canvas. Same renderer in editor, export and references.
3. **Panels edge cells (02b): the model fix is right; the rule is equal cells at even.** The spec says a gutter between cells and the same margin around (line 91), which means cells are equal when the dividers are "even". Dividers centered on a gutter with margin = gutter leave edge cells half a gutter wider. Define the even position per divider as the one that makes the cell sizes equal: x_k = m + k·c + (k − 0.5)·g with c = (1 − 2m − (n − 1)g) / n for n equal cells; snap and reset use it. At 1080 px the difference is 6 px, but "snap to even" must produce equal cells or users will see it in a 2 × 3 grid. A reference frame for a 3-column layout at even.
4. **Bubble scale 25 to 500%: accept.** Same as text. Two checks for 02b: at 25% the tail handle and the scale handle stay 48 dp targets (handles don't shrink with the bubble), and at 500% the bubble may leave the canvas (layers clip at the frame, P2-01).

## Fixes
**Must fix:** none.

**Polish**
- **P1 (before commit):** the slot hint fill over the Demotivational poster is `surface-raised` (above). Spec: P2-02 slot hint paragraph, amended.
- **P2:** at 200% Hindi (`50-hindi-200-classic-hints`) the two-line hint grows to about 80 dp and the bottom one runs to the canvas edge, where it looks cut; it also covers the photo's own caption. Keep every hint fully inside the frame (shift it in; don't crop it) and cap it at two lines. Spec line: the hint is "never wider than 90% of S" and sits in the slot; add "and inside the frame".
- **P3:** at 200% the Formats panel's hint line is cut mid-line at the panel's bottom (`50-spanish-200-formats-panel`, `50-hindi-200-formats-panel`). The panel is meant to scroll (40% cap); confirm on the device that it does and that the last line can be fully reached; a half-visible line with no scroll is a defect.
- **P4:** a README note, not a fix: the renders show the desktop stand-in font; the real fonts (Rozha, Poppins, Anton) and Hindi in Teko need the device check at phase QA.

## For the device pass (QA)
- Demotivational at 360 × 640 and 360 × 800 with the poster hint (`surface-raised`), then a title that wraps over the subtitle (the spec says the user scales it down; check it reads clearly).
- Ratio change with a format on: the photo and slots follow, a hand-placed photo keeps its place.
- Hindi 200%: Formats panel scrolls to its last line; hints inside the frame.
- Export parity of all four formats is closed at P2-08.

## Evidence
Renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P2-02/`; reference frames in `docs/qa/references/P2-02-formats/`; thought-bubble sizes from the spec's own radii (0.08 S × 1.0, 0.65, 0.4). Contrast values reused from the design system.

# Part b (Panels and Bubbles UI)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 30 desktop renders `60-*` and `61-*` (text is a stand-in; the README counts 48 for the whole ticket), the 12 reference frames, the 02a review and the handoff. The principal reviews the code separately.

## Verdict: Changes requested (two must-fixes, both small)

The layout and flows match the spec, and the 02a answers landed. M1 and M2 break spec lines and should be fixed before the commit; the rest is polish.

## What works
- **Equal cells at even** (`60-panels-cols3-even`): three columns measure the same (about 264 px each in the render), gutter and margin read evenly. The 3-column reference frame is the right one to keep.
- **Thought dots** (`61-bubbles-three-shapes`): three dots shrink toward the tip and read as a trail; the rule from 02a works.
- **Layout panel** (360 × 640): count tiles 2 to 6 and the three families fit without scrolling; selected tiles are white (selection). The main bar order is Formats, Layout, Text, Add photo, Layers; the Add bubble tool sits second in a bubbles meme, as the format tool.
- **Pick tile** (`Add photo` on an empty cell) is a dark `surface-raised` tile with the plus, readable on a white gutter background and on a photo; **File missing** tile uses the same look (P2-14's rule).
- **Divider panel and cell Position panel** use the stepper pattern (Move arrows, Even, Size minus and plus, Center): the single-pointer route for drag (SC 2.5.7) is there.
- **Bubbles:** the Style panel has Shape (three icon tiles), Font, Color, Fill; the Position panel has the Tail row; the Layers row shows the `bubble` icon and the first line as the name; locked and unlocked states match text layers.
- **200%** in es and hi: the Layout panel scrolls, Divider and Style panels hold, the cell bar wraps.

## Must fix
- **M1. The selected cell's ring is too faint to see, and white-on-white on a white canvas.** In `60-panels-grid4-cell-selected-bar`, `60-hindi-200-cell-bar` and `60-panels-grid4-cell-position-panel` the selected cell shows a hairline of about 1 dp where the spec says a 2 dp white ring with a near-black edge (two-tone), 4.3:1 or more over any color (spec contrast line 259; the cell selection rule in Layout). The default canvas is white, so a white ring outside the cell vanishes in the gutter, and the Replace, Position and Remove bar then acts on a cell the user can't identify. Fix: draw the two-tone ring 2 dp wide **inside** the cell's edge (over the picture), white with the `on-primary` 1 px edge on both sides, as for handles and text frames. Same for an empty cell selected.
- **M2. Editor-only labels are cut with an ellipsis at 200%.** `61-spanish-200-bubbles-main-bar`: the slot hint reads "Toca para añadir un boc…", and `60-spanish-200-divider-panel`: the pick tile reads "Añadir f…". Breaks the spec's 200% rule (line 260: labels wrap, nothing cut) and my 02a cap of two lines, which makes Spanish and Portuguese bubble hints impossible. Fix (spec amended): the hint wraps up to three lines, inside the frame, never an ellipsis; the pick tile wraps its label to two lines, or in a cell too small for that shows the plus alone with the full label as its spoken name.

## Polish
- **P1. Divider stays selected after Done (known limit).** Fix before QA: Done closes the panel and clears the selection (ToolPanel returns to the previous bar); leaving a highlighted divider and handles behind reads as a stuck state. Back and tap-away then need no special case.
- **P2. Selection frame doesn't follow a tail drag (known limit).** Accept for now. While dragging, either let the frame follow, or hide it until release; a frame that stays where the bubble used to reach is worse than none. One line in the handoff for QA's device pass either way.
- **P3. "Add bubble" wraps to two lines at 100% font** in `61-bubbles-three-shapes` (360 × 800), when "Add photo" fits. Shorten the tool label to **"Bubble"** (the tool is named by its object like "Text"; the panel title can stay "Bubble"); budget 10. The Spanish and Hindi 200% bars wrap either way and look fine.
- **P4. Layout panel: heading repeats the title.** The title is "Layout" and the group heading under the count tiles is also "Layout" (`60-panels-layout-panel-360x640`, `60-hindi-200-layout-panel`). Rename the heading to "Arrangement" (id es "Disposición", pt "Disposição") or drop it; the count tiles need no heading ("2 panels" spoken).
- **P5. At 25% the scale handle disappears under the tail handle** (`61-bubble-small-25-percent-selected`): the two touch and the tail handle wins, as specified, so a tiny bubble can't be enlarged from its corner. Pinch and the Position panel's Size still work, so it's not a dead end; improve by moving the scale-and-rotate handle to the nearest free corner when the two would overlap. Handles stay 48 dp.
- **P6. Style panel at 360 × 640 shows only Shape and Font** (`61-bubble-style-panel-360x640`); Color and Fill are below the 40% cap and found only by scrolling (the open watch item from P2-03). Accept, but QA notes whether testers find Fill; if not, Fill moves above Font for bubbles.
- **P7. "File missing" cell** (`60-panels-missing-cell`) still shows the picture behind its tile, though the spec says a cell with no file draws the gutter color. Likely the stand-in render; confirm on the device that the cell draws the background, not a stale bitmap.

## Fix check (7 Nov, refreshed `60-*` and `61-*` renders)
- **M1: fixed.** `60-panels-grid4-cell-selected-bar` shows a clear two-tone ring inside the cell edge, readable on the white gutter and over the picture.
- **M2: fixed.** `61-spanish-200-bubbles-main-bar` wraps "Toca para / añadir un / bocadillo" on three lines, no ellipsis, inside the frame. `60-spanish-200-divider-panel` shows the pick tile as the plus alone in the small cell. Device check: its spoken name is the full "Add photo" label.
- **P3 fixed** ("Bocadillo" fits one line at 200%), **P4 fixed** ("Arrangement").
- **P5: accepted as built.** At 25% (`61-bubble-small-25-percent-selected`) the two handles still touch, because every corner is within about 40 dp of the tail tip. Not a dead end: pinch and the Position panel's Size stepper enlarge it, and the tail handle winning the overlap is the spec's rule. If QA finds it fiddly, move the scale handle outside the frame, away from the tail.
- **Still open, unchanged:** P1 (Done clears the divider selection), P2 (frame during a tail drag), P6, P7 and the device-pass list below. None blocks the commit; P1 should land before QA.

## Final verdict: Approved with polish (part b)
Both must-fixes are closed. P1, P2, P6 and P7 go to the device pass.
