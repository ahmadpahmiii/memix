# Design review: P2-07 (background removal)

Reviewed 7 Nov 2026 from the 25 renders `97-*` (desktop, a stand-in ellipse mask, not ML Kit), the references in `docs/qa/references/P2-07-cutout/` (softness 0, 30, 100, a turned and cropped cut-out, at 1080 and 2160), the spec and the handoff, plus the `ic_cutout` drawable. Nothing ran on a device, so the real mask quality, the model download and the brush feel are for the device pass.

## Verdict: Changes requested (three must-fixes)

## What works
- **Ready** (`97-cutout-ready`, `-360x640`): the cut-out on the checkerboard, the "Background removed" pill, Erase and Restore (white selected), five brush sizes with Thin and Fine now distinct, Edge softness 30%, Save as sticker (Secondary) and Apply (the one blue), the privacy line. It fits at 360 × 640 with nothing cut.
- **Refine** (`erase-strokes`, `live-erase`, `restore-selected`): erased and restored strokes show at once; Remove cut-out is a Quiet blue text button above the actions; softness 0 and 100 differ clearly.
- **Cards:** Preparing with a determinate bar and the "once, needs the internet" body; the slow variant; Working; Offline, Can't remove, Memory and Nothing found all have Try again as the one blue action.
- **The layer:** the applied cut-out shows in the editor (selected bar: Position, Opacity, Delete, Edit photo, Crop, ...), the geometry references (`turned-cropped`) keep the mask with the picture, soft edges at 1080 and 2160 match scaled.
- **Known item 5** (the notice pill replaces "Background removed" for 3 s): accepted. Announce the notice politely, and bring the pill back after the 3 s.

## Must fix
- **M1. A literal backslash in the Nothing-found title.** `97-card-nothing-found` reads "We couldn\'t find a person, pet or object". The apostrophe escape reached the string. Check every string in the five languages for `\'` (the spec text in the PM's row used it too) and fix it in the resource. Compose resources need a plain `'` (the P1-02 review's note on `%` and `\uXXXX`).
- **M2. The status cards are clipped at 200% font.** In Spanish and Hindi (`97-card-offline-spanish-200`, `-hindi-200`) the card is taller than the stage, its body is cut after one line ("Memix necesita de…") and **Try again is not reachable**, which breaks "every failure has Try again" and the 200% rule (cards wrap, nothing is cut). The title row also takes two lines. Fix (spec amended, table row "Every status card"): the card scrolls inside the stage so title, body and Try again are always reachable. While the card shows, a `scrim` dims the picture (known item 1: the cards currently sit over an undimmed picture).
- **M3. Nothing found promises a brush that is not live.** The card says "or erase the background by hand" but the tools are inert (known item 2). Spec: the brush stays live on the whole picture. Either make the brush live (preferred: the spec's rule, and "Nothing found" must not be a dead end), or, if it can't be done in this build, change the body to "Try another photo." and say so in the handoff.

## Polish
- **P1. Layers-row thumbnail shows the whole picture** (known item 3; `97-layers-panel-cutout-row`): only the "Cut out" meta changes. Spec: the thumbnail is fitted inside with the cut-out applied. Land before QA; it is also the cue that tells a user which photo is cut out.
- **P2. Apply is cut at the bottom edge when "Remove cut-out" shows** (known item 4; `97-cutout-erase-strokes`): at 360 × 800 Apply and Save sit at the edge and the privacy line is below the fold. Pin the actions row and the privacy line at the bottom and scroll only the tools above them (spec amended). Apply is the primary and must never scroll away.
- **P3. The `ic_cutout` icon (new): accepted with one fix.** It reads as "a person" (head and shoulders), which is the right meaning, and the stroke grammar (2 dp, round joins) matches. Two differences from the spec: the head is a stroked ring where the drawing grammar and my spec call for a solid head over a stroked shoulders arc, and the head's outer edge touches the shoulders' (head to y 12 outer, shoulders start at 13 with their stroke), so there is no 2 dp clearance. Redraw: fill the head, lower the shoulders' top to about y 15. It does not appear in any frame (the main bar needs a scroll), so add an icon sheet render at 24 and 48 px to the references. Not a blocker.
- **P4. Hook name accepted.** `--es memix.bgRemoveFail <kind>` is better than the spec's `bg-remove-fail:<kind>`: it follows the other `memix.*` extras (`memix.exportWatermarkFree`, `memix.renderScene`). Spec updated (QA compares and Requests).
- **P5. The Hindi title** ("बैकग्राउंड हटाएं") and the Spanish title wrap to two lines at 200% and push the stage down. Accept, as the stage keeps its 30% minimum, but let the title take up to two lines and no more.
- **P6. Sticker toast.** `97-cutout-saved` shows "Saved to My stickers" on the stage as a toast: fine.

## Fix check (7 Nov)
- **M1 fixed.** `97-card-nothing-found`: "We couldn't find a person, pet or object" with a proper apostrophe.
- **M2 fixed.** The status cards dim the picture and scroll inside the stage (`97-card-offline-spanish-200`: title, then the body's first line, the card running to the stage edge, which cues the scroll; Try again is below it). Accepted: at 200% on 360 × 640 the stage is about 30% of the window and the pinned actions take the rest, as the spec allows. Device check: scroll to Try again with TalkBack and with a finger.
- **M3 fixed.** Nothing found is now a small card at the top, the whole picture stays visible and the brush is live (`97-card-nothing-found`, `-erased`).
- **P1, P2, P3 fixed.** The Layers thumbnail shows the cut-out; Save as sticker, Apply and the privacy line are pinned (`97-cutout-erase-strokes`); `ic_cutout` has a solid head, the shoulders clear of it, and reads at 24, 48 and 96 px beside Crop, Censor and Draw (`97-icon-sheet`).
- **Apply and Save while inert: no design-system change.** Adding a disabled look to Button would break a Memix rule (blocked controls keep their look and explain on tap, as Export, Crop and every other control). The gap is the silent tap: the controls must explain. Spec amended: a toast, "The cut-out isn't ready yet." while Preparing or Working, and "Erase part of the photo by hand first." in Nothing found with no stroke. Two new strings in five languages (`explain_cutout_not_ready`, `explain_cutout_nothing`). Small build item, not a blocker.

## Final verdict: Approved with polish
The explain-on-tap toasts and the device checks below remain.
