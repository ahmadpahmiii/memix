# Design review · P3-11 Region sheet

Reviewed 12 Nov 2026 against `docs/ux/specs/P3-11-region-sheet.md` and the OptionTile label layout. Evidence: the 22 desktop renders in `docs/ux/reviews/P3-11/` (Hindi uses a stand-in font; "संयुक्त राज्य" is the JVM's short name, accepted), the handoff, `RegionSheet.kt`, `RegionSheetViewModel.kt`, `ConfirmRegionUseCase.kt`, `HomeScreen.kt` (RegionButton), `SoundsBlocks.kt` (RegionHeader), the strings. No device, TalkBack, runtime language switch or motion was seen: those lines are read from code and spec, and QA must confirm them on the emulator.

## Final verdict (15 Nov, after the re-render): Pass with polish

Read-only language is gone: the list is never read-only (Android 13+ system per-app language, Android 10 to 12 a stored override with one recreate on Save), so `region_language_readonly` and its P1 are moot; on iOS the Language group is left out until P7-02 (spec updated). Fix check on the 21 re-renders and the code:
- **P2 (long name): fixed in code.** The name is `weight(1f, fill = false)`, `maxLines = 1`, and the design-system `Text` ellipsizes by default. No render shows a long name ("Estados Unidos" at 200%), so QA to confirm.
- **P3 (picker unknown body): fixed.** `SoundsBlocks.kt` line 365: in the picker the defensive state shows "No local sounds" with Browse global.
- **P4 (language row gutter): fixed.** `first-run-unknown`: Español has 22 dp clear on its right with the next tile peeking; `first-run-hi-200`: हिन्दी ends on the 16 dp gutter.
- **Home cards at font scale 1.5+: fixed.** `home-button-hi-200`: the two entry cards stack in one column and "वीडियो मीम" and "फ़ोटो मीम" no longer break inside a word.
- **P5 (new, one-line fix, must land before the ticket commit): Home's region button is no longer at the top right.** In `home-button-hi-200` and `first-run-unknown` it sits right after the wordmark (about x 473 to 680 of 900) instead of ending at the right gutter, where it was in the first renders. Cause: `HomeScreen.kt` `TopRow` gives the wordmark `weight(1f)` (fill) and the button `weight(1f, fill = false)`, so the wordmark takes half the row. Breaks spec line 45 ("the top-right of Home's top row") and puts the control mid-screen on the Home thumb path. Fix: wordmark with no weight, `horizontalArrangement = Arrangement.SpaceBetween` on the Row, the button keeps `weight(1f, fill = false)` so a long name still ellipsizes. Re-render `home-button` and `home-button-hi-200`.

No token changes. The sections below are the first-pass review, kept for the record; where they mention the read-only line or P1 to P4 as open, the lines above override them.

## First-pass verdict: Pass with polish

No must-fixes. The sheet is faithful to the spec, tokens-only, and all five builder questions are answered below. Four polish items (P1 to P4) should land before the phase-end QA; none blocks the ticket commit. Spec edits made: see "Spec changes".

## Answers to the five points

1. **New copy: approved, with one polish (P1).**
   - `home_region_change` "Change region" (13 chars, budget 24) is a click label only, never drawn. It repeats `sounds_region_change_a11y` in all five languages (Ubah wilayah, Cambiar región, Mudar região, क्षेत्र बदलें). Approved; added to the spec's copy table.
   - `region_language_readonly` "Memix follows your phone's language." (36 chars, budget 44): calm, plain, the five translations are within 44 (Hindi is the longest, about 42). Approved as the Android 12 and below fallback, but it ends in a dead end: it says why and not where to change it (principle 5, "errors say what to do next"). See P1. Added to the spec as a state.
2. **Spec conflict: my spec was wrong; corrected.** The PRD (line 247, PM 11 Nov) fires `region_change {language, region}` on a Save with a different language or region after first launch, never on open or dismiss. The builder's `ConfirmRegionUseCase` does exactly that, and `onboarding_complete` fires once on the first confirm. The spec's Analytics section now says so.
3. **Editor sound picker shows the region as a plain line: OK.** A second sheet over the picker over the editor would stack three layers on a 360 dp screen, and changing region mid-edit is rare (the first-run step already got it right for nearly everyone). `RegionHeader` with no `onOpen` keeps the row without the "Change" word and without a pressed fill, so it does not look tappable (checked in code: `clickable` is omitted). Conditions: (a) the user is never stranded, because the Local empty state keeps its "Browse global" Secondary and Global is one tab away; (b) see P3 for the one string that would lie there. Spec amended.
4. **Hindi `sounds_local_unknown_body`: matches the spec** ("लोकल साउंड देखने के लिए अपना क्षेत्र चुनें।"). Confirmed. One wording inconsistency is mine, not the builder's: Hindi mixes "लोकल साउंड" (body) and "स्थानीय टैब" (note, tab name). Flagged for the P5-06 translation pass; the tab is `स्थानीय`, so notes quoting the tab must say that.
5. **Read-only language on Android 12 and below: my view is that the list should never be read-only.** Reasons:
   - It breaks the very person Question 1 was written for: an Indonesian or Indian reader with an English phone gets English and no way out. Android 10 to 12 phones are the common phone in both markets (Assumption: no share data checked here; the PM or owner can look at the Play Console's Android version split after the first test track).
   - On those phones the sheet is titled "Region and language" and the first-run line says "You can change them later", which is false for the language.
   - It makes two classes of user: Android 13+ and iOS can pick, the rest cannot. The five-language promise is a launch headline (principle 7).
   - Guidance: per-app language is a platform feature from Android 13 only; below it the app has to do it itself ([Android per-app language preferences](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/resources/app-languages), which documents the AppCompat route for older versions). So an in-app override is the right design if the engineer can make Compose Multiplatform resources, the Hindi display face and `LocalLocale` follow it with a recreate. UX requirements for an override, so nothing else in the sheet changes: the same tiles and the same Save; selecting a language only applies on Save (as now); the sheet closes, then the activity recreates once (a brief flash is acceptable, and the project is not lost because the sheet is on browse screens and autosave covers editors; the language row is not reachable from inside an editor); the choice is stored by us, not read from the phone, and the phone's language is only the default.
   - If the override cannot land in P3: keep the read-only line as the fallback (the spec allows it), do P1, and log the override as a Phase 5 item for the PM ("language on Android 12 and below"). That is a scope proposal for the PM, not a decision I make.

## Checklist

**Tokens only: pass.** No `Color(0x`, `.dp`, `.sp`, `RoundedCornerShape(n)` or `FontFamily(` in `:feature:onboarding`, `:feature:home` or `:feature:sounds` (grep). Colors, spacing, shapes, `MemixSize.touchTarget`, `MemixStroke.strokeHairline` all from `MemixTheme`. Label layout uses `bodyStrong`, `onPrimary` on white, `text` on `surface-raised`. No outlines, no gradients, no v1 values. The Primary is the only blue in the sheet; the Local row's "Change" word and the Home chevron/globe follow the spec colors. No token changes.

**States (renders vs the spec table): pass.**
- First run, phone Indonesian: both selected, first-run line, "Lanjut"; at 360 x 640 the sheet fits with the Primary visible (`first-run-id-360x640`), as computed in the spec.
- Outside the five (`first-run-outside`): Philippines tile first and selected, note under the grid, English selected. Unknown region (`first-run-unknown`): Mexico pre-selected for Spanish with the calm note. Unsupported language (`first-run-fr`): English selected, "Memix isn't in French yet, so English is selected." All match the spec.
- Later open from Local and from Home (`later-from-local`, `later-from-home`): grabber, close button, Save, current values selected, backdrop dimmed.
- Local after change (`language-after-local-es`): "Sonidos para México", blue "Cambiar", list in the new language. Empty (`local-empty-outside`), unknown (`local-unknown-state`, "Choose your region", no Change word): both match.
- Read-only language (`later-readonly-language`): heading and one line in `text`; sheet otherwise unchanged. Acceptable (see point 5).
- Blocking first run: Back runs Confirm (`onDismiss`), no close button (`blocking = state.firstRun`), event once through `ConfirmRegionUseCase`. Confirm cannot double-fire (`confirming` guard).

**Copy and budgets in five languages: pass.** Every spec string is in the five `strings.xml`, within budget in the renders (the longest, the Indonesian note and the Hindi first-run line, wrap to two or three lines and are never cut). Tone: sentence case, no emoji, no exclamation marks.

**200% font: pass.** `first-run-hi-200` and `later-hi-200`: the grid is one column (the builder switched at 1.5x, earlier than the spec's 200%, which is better), the notes wrap, the middle scrolls, the language row holds Hindi in view and the Primary stays pinned and fully visible, one line "जारी रखें". Devanagari line height in the first-run line is generous and nothing is clipped. `home-button-hi-200`: the button holds "भारत" on one line (see P2).

**TalkBack: pass by code, not heard.** Title is a `Sheet` pane title; headings carry `heading()`; groups use `selectableGroup`, a `collectionInfo` and per-tile `collectionItemInfo` (positions "1 of 6"); the Region group is named by its heading; the Home button's `contentDescription` "Region: X" contains the visible name (SC 2.5.3) and its click label is "Change region"; the Local row merges to "Sounds for X, Change". The announcement is a polite live region on a real-size (hairline-high) node, which is the right fix for TalkBack ignoring zero-size nodes. QA to hear: Save announces "Region set to Mexico", focus returns to the opener, and after a language change focus lands on the opener.

**Motion: pass by code.** Sheet slide/fade is the shared Sheet component; the language switch waits `durationSheet` before applying so the sheet finishes closing (`RegionSheetViewModel.confirm`). QA to check the 200 ms Local cross-fade and reduce-motion.

## Polish

- **P1 (copy, optional if the override ships): say where to change the language.** Replace `region_language_readonly` with the same line plus a way out: "Memix follows your phone's language. Change it in your phone's settings." Budget 70. id "Memix mengikuti bahasa ponselmu. Ubah di pengaturan ponsel." es "Memix usa el idioma de tu teléfono. Cámbialo en los ajustes." pt "O Memix usa o idioma do seu celular. Mude nas configurações." hi "Memix आपके फ़ोन की भाषा के हिसाब से चलता है। इसे फ़ोन की सेटिंग में बदलें।" Breaks nothing in layout (the line already wraps). Breaks spec line: Principle 5 and the spec's "Forgiving" rule. If an override is built, delete the string.
- **P2 (200% font): Home's region button must not cut a long name.** `Text(..., maxLines = 1)` plus the wordmark on the same row: "Amerika Serikat", "Estados Unidos", "संयुक्त राज्य अमेरिका" at 200% will not fit beside "Memix". Spec line 45 says one line, but it does not say what gives. Decision: the name shrinks the wordmark's gap and then ellipsizes (`TextOverflow.Ellipsis`), with the full name in the spoken description (already so). Engineer to add the ellipsis and check "Estados Unidos" at 200% in the render. A cut-off name never matters for the control, since the globe and chevron stay and the sheet shows the full name.
- **P3 (copy in the picker): the unknown-region body must not tell the user to do what they cannot.** In the editor picker (no sheet), the defensive state would read "Pick your region to see local sounds." with no row to tap. Defensive only (a region always exists after the first run), but the engineer should give the picker the "See global sounds"/"Browse global" button there, or reuse the "No local sounds yet" body. A one-line change in the picker's empty block.
- **P4 (layout): the scrolling language row loses its gutter when it scrolls to the selected tile.** `first-run-unknown`: with Español selected, the row has shifted left by about 5 dp (English starts at 8 dp instead of 16 dp) and the selected tile ends 1 dp from the sheet's right edge, so it looks clipped. Spec line 33: "edge to edge, the selected one scrolled into view". Fix in `keptInViewWhen` or `ChoiceRow`: scroll so the selected tile has the sheet's `space-4` gutter on both sides (content padding at both ends of the row, not only at the start). Same fix applies to the Hindi renders, where the last tile ends 1 dp from the right edge.

## Observation outside this ticket (for the PM and the Home owner)

`language-after-home-hi` / `home-button-hi-200`: with Hindi at 200% the Home entry card title breaks inside a word ("वीडिय" then "ो मीम"), leaving an orphaned matra. The stand-in font may exaggerate it, but any engine will break mid-word on a half-width card at that scale. This has only become reachable now that the language can change in-app. Request for the P0-05 build (a Home polish, not a P3-11 must-fix): at font scale 1.5 and above, stack the two entry cards in one column, or reduce the Anton/Teko title one step, so a word is never broken. QA to add Hindi 200% on Home to the phase-end list.
