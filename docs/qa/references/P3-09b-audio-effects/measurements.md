# P3-09b reference renders

Generated by the P3-09b harness through `AudioChain` (the chain preview and export both build; no volume change). Mono, 22.05 kHz, 16-bit. `<id>.wav` is the result, `<id>.png` plots the source (above), the result (middle) and the spectrum of both (grey source, blue result, 20 Hz to 10 kHz, dB re full scale) below. The echo and cave renders are longer than their source: the tail rings out (`SoundClip.tailUs`, at most 1.5 s). Android renders them with Media3 1.11.1; iOS compares by ear and by these numbers. The limiter's ceiling is -1 dBFS: no render with an effect that can raise the level has a peak above it.
