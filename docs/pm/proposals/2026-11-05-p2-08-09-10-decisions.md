# P2-08, P2-09 and P2-10: watermark on photos, the GIF, and what "Make it a video meme" carries

**Date:** 5 Nov 2026 · **Author:** PM (ready checks for P2-08, P2-09, P2-10) · **Status:** **Approved by delegation (owner, 2 Nov): recommendation applied, owner reviews after Phase 2** (1A, 2A, 3B)

## 1. Does the watermark go on photo exports?

**Problem:** the PRD says the "Memix" mark goes on "every free export", and P1-13 built it for video only. A photo export has PNG, JPG, a clipboard copy and WhatsApp stickers.
- **A. On PNG, JPG and clipboard copies; not on WhatsApp stickers (recommended).** Same top-left mark, 3% of the shorter side, absent when the export is marked watermark-free (P5-02's rewarded ad). A 512 px sticker with a mark would be spoiled, and asking for an ad per sticker is absurd. Cost: stickers carry no brand; reverse: add the mark to stickers in a later ticket.
- **B. On everything, stickers included.** Literal reading of "every", but stickers become unusable.
- **C. On nothing in photos.** Gives up the free brand reach photos carry on every share.

**Recommendation: A.**

## 2. GIF export

**Problem:** P2-08 lists "GIF for animated layers". No animated layer can exist before P4-12 (animated stickers; today a GIF or WebP sticker draws its first frame), so the GIF option could neither be shown nor checked.
- **A. Move GIF export to P4-12 (recommended).** P2-08 ships PNG, JPG, clipboard, album; P4-12's ticket text gains "photo GIF export" and its Needs gain P2-08. Cost: none now; the PRD's GIF promise is kept by launch.
- **B. Build the encoder now, hide the option.** An unused, unchecked path.
- **C. Drop GIF.** Breaks a PRD promise.

**Recommendation: A.** Applied: TICKETS P2-08 and P4-12.

## 3. What "Make it a video meme" carries (P2-10)

**Problem:** the PRD says the photo opens in the video editor "as a clip with its layers", and the Done when says every layer keeps position, size and style. The video engine does not render overlay (P4-01) or sticker (P4-12) tracks until Phase 4, and has no equivalent for drawings, censors, image edits or formats.
- **A. Bake everything into one picture.** Looks exactly like the photo; every layer, text included, becomes pixels, so nothing is editable in the video editor.
- **B. Bake the scene, but keep the top text layers as video captions (recommended).** The photo (with formats, images, stickers, drawings, censors, edits and any text that has other layers above it) is drawn by the scene function into one picture used as the 3-second clip. Text layers at the top of the stack (fully opaque) become real captions (same style, same position, same renderer), so words stay editable and can be timed and animated later. Order stays right because only text that nothing covers is lifted.
- **C. Wait until Phase 4 and map layers to tracks.** Truest to the PRD, but P2-10 moves out of Phase 2 and the Phase 2 goal ("Make it a video meme") is not met.

**Recommendation: B.** Reverse: after P4-01 and P4-12 a later ticket can map image and sticker layers to tracks instead of baking them; saved video drafts are unaffected.

## Impact

No launch date moves. Phase 2 stays planned for 16 Nov–6 Dec.
